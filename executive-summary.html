Key findings

  • Two live major series. DeepStack Extravaganza IV at The Venetian (Nov 16 – Dec 23, $3.6M+ guaranteed) and WPT World Championship at Wynn (Nov 27 – Dec 21, $20M+ guaranteed, 85+ events). WSOP 2026 and WSOP Circuit have concluded.
  • Best single-entry value. $600 NLH $150K GTD (Venetian, Dec 20–23), $800 NLH $500K GTD (Venetian, Dec 3–6), $600 NLH $400K GTD (Wynn, Nov 27–30). Biggest guarantee: WPT Prime Championship $5,000,000 GTD ($1,100, Dec 5–12). Flagship: WPT World Championship $10,400 (Dec 13–20).
  • Best hotels. Stay at the venue to maximize playing time: Wynn/Encore (WPT World Championship, 10–15 min walk to Venetian) or The Venetian/Palazzo (DeepStack, on-site). Resort fee $55+tax (~$62.36) at both; Wynn self-parking is free for guests, Venetian charges $20/day.
  • True cost per night = base rate + resort fee + 13.38% Clark County tax + parking. Example: Wynn at a $176 sample base ≈ $261.91/night; Venetian at $200 sample ≈ $309.12/night (includes $20 parking). Sample rates are not verified from official sources.
  • All distances are walkable. Venetian↔Wynn 0.5 mi (10–15 min); Venetian↔Caesars ~0.7 mi (~14 min); Venetian↔Bellagio ~0.9 mi (18–25 min). All within the 30-minute requirement.
  • Keep rebuy/add-on out of the single-entry plan. Wynn Friday $240 rebuy, Caesars $50 add-on, and Wynn Super Gold Million unlimited re-entry are listed separately (Section B).
