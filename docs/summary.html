Executive summary

Important details first: what is verified, what is excluded, and what is still missing.

Bottom line

  • 47 single-entry events are listed at the Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16 - Dec 23, 2026) on the official Venetian schedule. Of these, 24 have a structure sheet reviewed in this session; the rest are flagged for line-by-line confirmation.
  • 17 events are excluded from the single-entry list: multi-flight events (27 rows), satellites (7 rows), one team event, and one restricted-eligibility event. Day-2 restarts are listed separately as continuations.
  • The WPT World Championship festival at Wynn (136 official schedule rows) is treated as multi-entry: every Wynn structure sheet reviewed states unlimited re-entry or one re-entry. The $10,400 WPT World Championship structure was not found, so it is marked unverified.
  • Best single-entry value by guarantee per buy-in (computed from official figures): $200 NLH PokerAtlas Valuetown Bounty $20K GTD (1Day) (Sat Nov 28, 2026, 5:10 PM); $200 NLH PokerAtlas Valuetown Bounty $20K GTD (1Day) (Sat Dec 19, 2026, 5:10 PM); $800 No Limit Hold'em Bounty $75K GTD (1Day) (Wed Dec 09, 2026, 11:10 AM).
  • 8:00 AM start: not achievable from verified data. The earliest single-entry start found is 10:10 AM. No official 8:00 AM single-entry event was found.
  • Hotels: only resort fees are verified. The Venetian and Wynn both list a $55 plus tax resort fee on official pages. Nightly rates, the Clark County tax rate and walking times are not verified, so no hotel ranking is possible yet.
  • Coverage gap: other Strip rooms (Bellagio, MGM Grand, Caesars, Resorts World, Horseshoe/Paris, etc.) and the ARIA Poker Open have not been verified from official schedules.
