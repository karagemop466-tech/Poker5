Analysis and findings

What the verified data says, and what it cannot say yet.

Method

  • Single-entry filter: single day, one starting flight, no re-entry/rebuy/add-on on the official schedule, not a satellite, team, or Day-2 restart.
  • Value metric: guarantee ÷ buy-in (computed). It is a simple proxy for how much the organiser promises per dollar. It ignores field size and the house fee. The prize pool per buy-in (from structure sheets) is shown where reviewed (e.g., 80% of a $200 buy-in goes to the prize pool; 85% of $600).
  • Cost: buy-in only. Hotel cost is not included because no nightly rate is verified.

Findings

  • Best guarantee per buy-in (single-entry): $200 NLH PokerAtlas Valuetown Bounty $20K GTD (1Day) (100.0x, Sat Nov 28, 2026), $200 NLH PokerAtlas Valuetown Bounty $20K GTD (1Day) (100.0x, Sat Dec 19, 2026), $800 No Limit Hold'em Bounty $75K GTD (1Day) (93.8x, Wed Dec 09, 2026), $600 No Limit Hold'em Bounty $50K GTD (1Day) (83.3x, Wed Dec 16, 2026).
  • Lowest-cost single-entry events: $200 NLH PokerAtlas Valuetown Bounty $20K GTD (1Day) on Sat Nov 28, 2026, $200 NLH PokerAtlas Valuetown Bounty $20K GTD (1Day) on Sat Dec 19, 2026, $200 No Limit Hold'em $8K GTD (1Day) on Mon Nov 23, 2026.
  • Bounty events: many single-entry events are bounty tournaments. The bounty money is part of the guarantee, so the cash prize pool for those events is smaller than the headline number. Treat bounty GTDs with care.
  • Age limit: the Seniors events are 50+ only; they are included but flagged.
  • Schedule shape: single-entry events cluster at 11:10 AM, 12:10 PM, 10:10 AM and 5:10 PM. 14 days have two or more single-entry starts.
  • WPT: none of the reviewed WPT formats is single-entry, so the WPT festival (the largest guarantees) is outside the single-entry list.

What the data does not yet support

  • A ranking by expected value (field size and payout structure per event are not verified).
  • An all-day plan starting at 8:00 AM (no official 8:00 AM single-entry start found).
  • A hotel ranking (no verified nightly rate or walking time).
Figures are as published by the named official source on 2026-11-16. Unverified values are left blank and flagged. Not financial advice.
