Findings

  • All 30 verified single-entry events are at The Venetian Poker Room (DeepStack Extravaganza IV). No other single-entry venue was verified.
  • The lowest verified buy-in tier is $200: 9 single-entry events, with $8,000 to $20,000 guarantees. Bounties are paid to eliminators, so each player's prize depends on the field.
  • Wynn Las Vegas daily events carry a higher buy-in-to-guarantee profile (e.g., $25,000 GTD weekend at $200) but are multi-entry (re-entry allowed), so they are not in the single-entry list.
  • The Venetian's own poker-suite rate requires a $300+ tournament buy-in each night (or 6 hours of live cash). Verified $300+ single-entry events: 21 of 30.

Calculated values: multiples and sums are arithmetic on the verified guarantees and buy-ins. Sum includes bounty money where applicable.
