Hotel recommendation
  • If distance/convenience is top priority: The Venetian or Palazzo (0–2 min walk). Walk to every tournament.
  • If cost/quality is top priority: compute the true all-in cost for Caesars Palace, Paris, The LINQ, Bellagio, ARIA, or Wynn/Encore (all within 30 min walk) using rate + resort fee + ~13.38% tax, then compare.
  • Mandatory resort fees (~$45–$55) and ~13.38% tax can add roughly $65–$75+/night on top of the advertised rate.
What is NOT verified — read before deciding
  • Single-entry status for (1Day) events is a structural interpretation of official structure sheets (one starting flight, no re-entry language) — not an explicit "single entry" label. Confirm with the Venetian Poker Room.
  • Hotel nightly rates are not confirmed from any official source (dynamic).
  • Resort fees confirmed officially only at Wynn ($45 or $55 by offer); all others from third-party aggregators — unverified vs official.
  • Other Strip rooms (Wynn WPT, MGM Grand, Aria, Bellagio, Circa) seen only on third-party aggregators — not in verified tables.
  • No 8 AM start at the Venetian; earliest is 10:10 AM.
Next steps before booking
  1. Confirm each tournament's entry rules (single entry vs re-entry) directly with the Venetian Poker Room.
  2. Get live nightly rates + exact resort fee from each hotel's official booking page for your dates; compute true cost/night.
  3. Verify walking times with a directions tool (must be ≤30 min).
  4. Review the Flagged for Review page and Open Questions before any booking decision.
