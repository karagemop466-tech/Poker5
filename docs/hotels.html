How to compute the true cost (do this before booking)
  1. Pick your exact check-in/check-out dates (within Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026).
  2. Go to the hotel's official site (linked above) and record the nightly room rate before fees/tax.
  3. Add the mandatory resort fee — confirm the exact amount on the official booking page.
  4. Add Clark County room tax of ~13.38% (applies to rate + resort fee).
  5. True cost per night = (nightly rate + resort fee) × 1.1338.
  6. Total stay cost = true cost per night × number of nights (add parking only if you drive — not needed if walking).

Hotel recommendation

Convenience: Stay at The Venetian or Palazzo (0–2 min walk) — you can walk to every tournament, no matter how early or late.

Cost/quality: Compute the true all-in cost for Caesars Palace, Paris, The LINQ, Bellagio, ARIA, or Wynn/Encore using the steps above and compare. All are within a 30-minute walk. Do not assume the advertised nightly rate is what you pay — mandatory resort fees (~$45–$55) and ~13.38% tax can add roughly $65–$75+/night.
