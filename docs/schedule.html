Single-entry schedule (Venetian, Nov 16 to Dec 9, 2026)

8 AM all-day plan: not possible with single-entry events alone. The earliest verified single-entry start is 11:10 AM ($300 No-Limit Hold'em (1-Day), Wednesday, Nov 18), and the RaceDay stack (10:10 AM, Nov 19 to 21) is the only 10:10 AM start. Most other days start at 11:10 AM. Evening starts are at 5:10 PM.

To play more events on a single day, you would need to add re-entry or add-on events from the Separate section. Those include Caesars Palace at 10 AM, 2 PM, 6 PM and 9 PM daily; Wynn at noon and 6 PM; and Bellagio on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays at 11 AM. Walking times between venues are not verified (see the Flagged page).
