What this site covers

Live, upcoming poker tournaments on or near the Las Vegas Strip, filtered to single-entry events. Events that allow a second or later starting-flight entry, a re-entry, a rebuy or an add-on are kept in a separate section, as requested. Every table row links to the official source it came from.

Pages

  • Executive summary - the short version: what is verified, what is not, and the answer to the 8:00 AM all-day question.
  • Single-entry tournaments - all 47 Venetian single-entry events, ranked by guarantee-to-buy-in ratio.
  • Daily schedule - single-entry options by day, with the days that have two or more shots.
  • Excluded - multi-flight, satellites, the team event, and Day-2 continuation rows.
  • WPT / Wynn festival - the full official Wynn schedule. Almost all of it is multi-entry.
  • Hotels - official resort fees only; nightly rates and walking times are not verified yet.
  • Analysis - method, findings, and what the data does and does not support.
  • Questions to answer - what must be known before a decision can be made, and where to get it.
  • Suggestions - site improvements for review before anything is changed.
  • Flagged for Review - irregularities and unverified items, with source links.
  • Sources & repo review - every source URL used, and the repository review.
Important: No hotel can yet be recommended on verified numbers. Nightly rates and walking times are not available from official sources in this session. See Hotels and Questions.
