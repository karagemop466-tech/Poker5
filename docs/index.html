Executive summary

Bottom line. Only the Venetian (Palazzo) runs single-entry events that can be verified from official documents in this window. 20 single-entry events are listed (Nov 18 to Dec 7, 2026). Caesars Palace, Wynn and Bellagio events all allow re-entry, rebuy or an add-on, so they are in the separate section. ARIA and Horseshoe publish no schedule, and Planet Hollywood and Resorts World are reported closed.

Key findings

  • Best single-entry value. The $600 events on Dec 6 (Event 28) and Dec 7 (Event 30) carry $40,000 guarantees. Event 14 (Nov 27) carries $30,000. Prize pool share of buy-in is 85% for the $600 events (official structure sheet: $510 of $600).
  • An all-day plan from 8 AM is not possible with single-entry events alone. The earliest verified single-entry start is 10:10 AM (RaceDay stack, Nov 19 to 21). Single-entry starts are at 10:10 AM (RaceDay), 11:10 AM and 5:10 PM. Most days have one or two events.
  • Re-entry is common on the Strip. Caesars Palace (all four dailies), Wynn (all dailies), and Bellagio (all three weeklies) allow re-entry, rebuy or add-ons. They are kept in the separate section so you can decide.
  • Poker-suite rate at the Venetian. The official rate starts at "as low as $119 (Venetian) and $209 (Palazzo)", but it requires a $300+ tournament buy-in or 6 hours of live cash play each night. Single-entry events at $300 or more are listed on the Schedule page.
  • Hotel costs are only partly verifiable. The Venetian/Palazzo and Wynn resort fee is $55 plus tax per night on official terms. Nightly rates and walking times are blank because they could not be confirmed from official sources.
  • Planet Hollywood and Resorts World are reported closed (trade press and PokerAtlas). Official confirmation was not found, so both are excluded and flagged.
