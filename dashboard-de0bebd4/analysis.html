Findings

  • Bounties inflate the headline. 27 of the 30 single-entry events are bounty or PKO formats; their guarantees include bounty money per the official sheets. Read the cash prize pool, not the headline, when comparing.
  • House fees on the sheets read are 11.8%–20.0% of buy-in (e.g., $40 of $200, $90 of $600, $130 of $1,100). The 12.5% figure on the sheets is the share of the field paid, not a fee.
  • Largest verified guarantee: #36 ($75,000) on Wed Dec 9, 2026; the $1,100 PKO at #52 has the same $75,000 guarantee. The $800 bounty #36 has the highest guarantee ÷ buy-in among verified singles.
  • Multi-flight is where the money is, but it is not single-entry. Venetian's 1A–1F events (e.g., #26 $800 with $500K guarantee; #56 $1,100 with $500K; #47 $2,200 with $1M) are excluded here because the structure sheets say players can play later flights.
  • Wynn's dailies are cheap but re-entry. $200 NLH, $10K–$25K guarantees, re-entries to about 4:30 PM. The Friday $240 event is a rebuy and add-on event.
  • No verified tournament starts before 11:10 AM. The 9:10 AM Venetian event (#46) is a satellite, not a cash entry.

Limits of this analysis

  • Only the official pages listed on the Sources page were used. Resorts World, Caesars/Planet Hollywood and Horseshoe/Paris tournament schedules were not confirmed from official pages.
  • Venetian structure sheets read: 1, 7, 10, 11, 15, 16, 33, 34. Every single-entry event maps to one of these sheets (0 single-entry events use a sheet not read).
  • WSOP (official wsop.com: May 26 – July 15, 2026) is outside the Nov–Dec window and is excluded.
