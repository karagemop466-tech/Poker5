# Tournament research — Las Vegas Strip area

**Checked:** 2026-11-16 (date supplied by the working environment)  
**Scope:** Published events from 2026-11-17 through 2026-12-23, plus currently published recurring schedules for nearby poker rooms. Times below are copied as published; the room's local-time zone is not printed in every source.  
**Rule:** An event is listed as a qualifying single-entry option only if an official event rule explicitly limits the player to one entry and does not offer re-entry, rebuy, add-on, or paid extra chips. A one-day label, a tournament title, or silence about re-entry is not proof of single-entry.

## Executive result

- **No upcoming event could be verified as satisfying the strict single-entry/no-paid-advantage filter from the official material checked.** This is not a claim that no such events exist; it means the sources reviewed do not publish enough explicit entry-limit language to certify any as eligible.
- **Most promising quantified low/mid-buy-in candidates to verify with the room:** Venetian's $300 NLH $10,000 guarantee (Nov. 18); the $400 NLH $75,000 guarantee (Nov. 23–25); and the $800 NLH $500,000 guarantee (Dec. 3–6). The structure sheets state dates, buy-in/guarantee, and some flight rules, but do not explicitly cap each player at one entry. They therefore stay out of the verified-single-entry list.
- **Largest clearly quantified guarantee under the Wynn World Poker Tour schedule:** $1,100 WPT Prime Championship, $5,000,000 guarantee, Dec. 5–12. Its official terms allow one entry **and one re-entry per starting flight**, so it fails the requested single-entry rule.
- **8:00 a.m. start:** none was found in the official schedules checked. Caesars' own tournament article lists Horseshoe's earliest daily start as 9:00 a.m.; it does not publish the corresponding buy-in or entry policy. Do not plan on an 8:00 a.m. tournament without a newly verified schedule.
- **A full day of starts is not the same as a full day of completed tournaments.** Caesars says most tournament sessions last four to six hours. The 9:00, 10:00, 11:00 a.m. and noon starts overlap in play; a player still alive cannot safely schedule another event at the next start. A conservative itinerary needs the exact blind structure, expected duration, registration cut-off and walking time.

## Event shortlist and value calculation

The attached `data/tournaments.json` contains the row-level, source-linked shortlist. It is deliberately a **curated set of strong or low-cost candidates**, not a transcription of every side event in each series. The full official calendars linked above contain the complete event lists.

For events with an official guarantee, the site may display a simple comparison index:

> **Guarantee multiple = advertised event guarantee ÷ one advertised buy-in**

This is arithmetic on published values, **not** expected value, not a per-entry payout promise, and not a probability of cashing. It can mislead when the event has multiple flights, re-entry, a bounty allocation, or an added-prize condition. The comparison is therefore informational only and is never used to call an event a verified single-entry tournament.

Strongest values among the fully published figures reviewed:

- **WPT Prime Championship — $1,100 / $5,000,000 guarantee; 4,545× index.** Official terms allow one entry and one re-entry for each starting flight. It is not single-entry.
- **WPT $800 NLH — $800 / $1,000,000 guarantee; 1,250× index.** Official structure permits unlimited re-entry for each starting flight. It is not single-entry.
- **WPT $600 NLH — $600 / $400,000 guarantee; 667× index.** Official structure permits unlimited re-entry for each starting flight. It is not single-entry.
- **Venetian $800 NLH — $800 / $500,000 guarantee; 625× index.** Six flights are listed. The structure sheet restricts later flights after bagging but does not explicitly publish the maximum entries before bagging; not verified as single-entry.
- **Venetian $1,100 NLH — $1,100 / $500,000 guarantee; 455× index.** Five flights and a first-flight early-registration fee discount are listed. Entry maximum before bagging is not stated; not verified as single-entry.
- **Venetian $2,200 NLH — $2,200 / $1,000,000 guarantee; 455× index.** Two flights are listed. Entry maximum before bagging is not stated; not verified as single-entry.
- **Venetian $600 NLH — $600 / $150,000 guarantee; 250× index.** Three flights and a first-flight early-registration fee discount are listed. Entry maximum before bagging is not stated; not verified as single-entry.
- **Venetian $400 NLH — $400 / $75,000 guarantee; 188× index.** Two- or three-flight versions are listed. The structures do not state the maximum entries before bagging; not verified as single-entry.
- **Caution: WPT $400 Super Gold Million Mystery Bounty — $400 / $3,000,000 advertised guarantee; 7,500× arithmetic index, but not ranked as a confirmed local prize-pool value.** The official WPT schedule footnote says $1,000,000 of cash prizes will be added by ClubWPT Gold and says ClubWPT Gold is not available in Nevada. The official materials reviewed do not clearly resolve the Nevada participant's applicable prize-pool amount or state the event's re-entry cap. Get written confirmation from Wynn before comparing or entering.

The data is not a full tournament universe: recurring Caesars, Horseshoe, MGM Grand and ARIA events have incomplete current terms in the official pages reviewed, so they cannot be used as strict-filter recommendations. They are kept in separate, clearly marked rows in the data file.

## Spend-more / multiple-entry section

These are kept separate from the requested single-entry shortlist because official terms explicitly allow another paid entry, a rebuy, an add-on, or a paid chip advantage:

1. **Wynn daily $200 Monday–Thursday, $10,000 guarantee, noon:** registration and re-entries run until Level 9. This is multiple-entry, not strict single-entry.
2. **Wynn daily $200 Saturday/Sunday, $25,000 guarantee, noon:** re-entries and one $100 add-on for 25,000 chips are allowed.
3. **Wynn nightly $160, $10,000 guarantee, 6 p.m.:** re-entries and one $100 add-on for 25,000 chips are allowed.
4. **Wynn Friday $240, $40,000 guarantee, noon:** the rules prohibit re-entry but allow unlimited $200 rebuys when a player's stack is at or below 15,000 and one $100 add-on for 15,000 chips. This is not single-entry in the user's requested economic sense.
5. **WPT $600 / $400,000 and $800 / $1,000,000 multi-flight events:** official structures allow unlimited re-entry per starting flight until the stated level.
6. **WPT Prime $1,100 / $5,000,000:** one entry plus one re-entry per starting flight.
7. **Caesars Palace $100 / $1,000 daily:** the official Caesars article states that a $50 add-on at the first break buys 15,000 more chips. Re-entry limit is not stated in that article.
8. **MGM Grand $120 structure:** the linked PDF states a voluntary $11 staff gratuity grants 10,000 extra chips and that players with five daily entries receive a free Saturday entry. The linked schedule PDF is headed “August Tournament Schedule” without a year, so those buy-in/extra-stack terms are **not treated as current November 2026 facts**. This mismatch is flagged rather than silently carried forward.

## Irregularities flagged for review

- **No verified strict single-entry option:** do not interpret an omitted re-entry clause as “no re-entry.”
- **Venetian multi-flight policy gap:** current official rules state who cannot take a later flight after bagging, but the reviewed sheets do not state a numeric maximum before that point.
- **WPT Super Gold Million Nevada footnote:** $1 million added by ClubWPT Gold is mentioned, and that service is stated unavailable in Nevada. The local applicable guarantee is unclear from the reviewed public schedule.
- **WPT schedule PDF date/day mismatch:** the extracted official schedule text labels a Dec. 18 entry “Wednesday,” although Dec. 18, 2026 is Friday, and nearby rows are not consistently ordered. Rows using those ambiguous December 16–18 labels are not relied upon here; confirm directly against Wynn's current event calendar before planning those dates.
- **MGM linked PDF appears stale/undated:** it is labeled “August Tournament Schedule” while the poker-room page is current in November 2026. Buy-in and optional extra-chip details from that PDF are not treated as current facts.
- **Bellagio page has no usable future date:** its “Saturday, July 26” tournament reference omits a year and does not establish an upcoming event as of Nov. 16, 2026.
- **Room-level status and seat availability are not guaranteed:** published schedules can be changed by management; confirm event operation, registration cutoff, and seat availability directly.
