Executive summary

Status as of 16 Nov 2026 (UTC). What is verified, what is not, and what you need to decide. Blank = not verified.

What is verified

  • 36 single-entry live tournaments at The Venetian Poker Room, Nov 18 – Dec 23, 2026. Each row lists the official schedule and the structure sheet checked. Every date, time, buy-in, and guarantee was re-checked against the official schedule and event page (verification log). See the list.
  • Highest guarantee-to-buy-in multiples (calculated): the $200 Valuetown Bounty on Nov 28 ($20K GTD) and the $800 Bounty on Dec 9 ($75K GTD). Bounty guarantees include bounty money on the sheets checked.
  • Multi-entry and flight formats are kept separate: Wynn daily tournaments (re-entry and add-on formats) and Venetian flight events. See the separate section.
  • Resort fees (official): The Venetian $55 per night, per the official poker-suite page. Wynn is offer-specific: $45 + tax on some offers and $55 + tax on others. The Palazzo, Encore, Caesars, and MGM fees are NOT verified.

What is not verified (left blank)

  • Nightly room rates for any dates. The Venetian page gives "as low as" $119 and $209 with no dates or mapping.
  • Walking time from any hotel to the Venetian. No official walking distance was found.
  • Room tax: 13.38% is from third-party guides only; the official Clark County page was not retrieved.
  • Caesars Palace and MGM resort fees: conflicting or third-party only.
  • Single-entry status of Venetian events whose structure sheets were not checked (see Flagged P-04), and the full Wynn WPT World Championship schedule (P-01).

Key constraints for planning

  • The earliest verified single-entry start is 11:10 AM, so an 8 AM start with verified single-entry events is not possible. See the Day plan.
  • The verified Venetian events require 21+ and a Venetian Rewards card (per the structure sheets checked).
  • The Venetian DSE series (Nov 16 – Dec 23) is the only single-entry source verified in full. The Wynn WPT World Championship (Nov 27 – Dec 21) is a large festival whose single-entry rules still need checking.
