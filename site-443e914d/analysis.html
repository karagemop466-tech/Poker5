Findings

  • All 36 single-entry events are at The Venetian Poker Room (DeepStack Extravaganza IV). No other single-entry venue was verified.
  • The lowest verified buy-in tier is $200: 14 single-entry events, with $8,000 to $20,000 guarantees. Bounties are paid to eliminators, so each player's prize depends on the field.

  • The Venetian's poker-suite rate requires 6 hours of live cash or a $300+ tournament buy-in each night (official). Single-entry events at $300 or more: 22 of 36.
