Executive summary

  1. Single-entry gate: nothing passes. The official Venetian schedule repeats event numbers across flights (multi-flight, 39 rows). The rest of the Venetian rows read by us are silent on re-entry, which is not the same as single-entry. WPT Wynn terms for the $600 NLH ($400K), $600 TORSE and $400 PLO allow unlimited re-entry until level 9.
  2. Ranking is withheld. The brief ranks only after the total entry fee and guarantee are confirmed and the event passes single-entry. Guarantee ÷ buy-in is shown as a descriptive ratio only.
  3. Hotels. The Venetian resort fee is verified: $55 plus tax per night (official page). The Wynn fee could not be verified. Nightly rates and walking times were not retrieved from official sources, so they are blank.
  4. Tax. The Clark County schedule sets 13.38% for a resort hotel in the primary gaming corridor. That text is the 2017 ordinance and must be reconfirmed. Whether the Venetian lies inside the corridor is not checked against the map.
  5. Calculator. Enter entries, buy-ins, nights and your own rates. It applies tax to rate + mandatory resort fee, which the ordinance's 'rent' definition supports (see Flagged F7).
  6. Monitoring. A scheduled job re-reads the official URLs each morning, stores a hash, and flags any change. It has not yet run on GitHub.
