## What is confirmed single-entry

A row earns the *Confirmed single-entry* label only when an official source states
a one-entry limit. All 37 official Venetian structure sheets were read on 2026-11-17;
none contains a one-entry, no-re-entry or freezeout statement, and every WPT / Wynn
event whose terms were read permits re-entry. **No event is labelled single-entry on
this site.** The 47 entry rows whose sheet shows no re-entry, rebuy or add-on text
are the closest candidates; they stay labelled *Entry cap not stated*, because a
silence on entry limits is not evidence of a freezeout. The standard is published on
the site's Method & sources page so it can be challenged.

## What is deliberately blank

* **Room rates** — dynamic; stored as a dated log of booking-page snapshots, never
  as one fixed value.
* **Off-property walking times** — no casino publishes one; only on-property routes
  (0 minutes) pass the 30-minute filter.
* **Finishing times** — not published; the site derives a playing-day length from
  each sheet's late-registration level count and labels it as derived.
* **Caesars Palace / Horseshoe resort fees** — no official page found.
* **Bellagio, MGM Grand, ARIA tournament rows** — the official material is undated,
  stale, or absent.
