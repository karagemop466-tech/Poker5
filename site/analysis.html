Analysis

Analysis uses only verified single-entry Venetian events. The guarantee-to-buy-in ratio is a calculation: guarantee ÷ buy-in. Breakeven entries is also a calculation: guarantee ÷ prize pool per entry from the structure sheet. A guarantee is paid even if the field is small, so a higher breakeven count means more risk that the event runs as an overlay.

Findings

  • The highest prize pools are at the Venetian: $1,000,000 for the $2,200 PokerAtlas event (1A on Dec 12, 10:10 AM) and $500,000 for the $800 event (#26) and $1,100 event (#56). The 1A flights are verified single entry.
  • The best value per dollar is the $800 event (#26, GTD ÷ buy-in 625x) and the $300 event #1 (250x). These are verified single entry and also have large field-size risk; check the breakeven counts.
  • Verified bounty events ($200 to $300) have $8,000 to $15,000 guarantees. Their GTD ÷ buy-in is 40x to 50x, below the $75,000-guarantee events, but their buy-ins are much lower.
  • Wynn's cheapest daily events ($200 with $10,000 to $25,000 guarantees) and Caesars' $100 daily events all allow re-entry or add-ons, so they cannot be ranked as single entry.
  • Bellagio's Friday and Sunday events allow re-entry and sell extra chips for a staff gratuity, so they are excluded from single entry.

Method and limits

  • Single-entry status is checked only from each event's official structure sheet. Events whose sheets were not read are shown as unverified.
  • Prize pool per entry is taken from the structure sheet. Two sheets conflict on house fees for the same buy-in: the $300 sheets say $242 and $240 per entry, and the $400 sheets say $330 and $335. Both figures are shown in Flagged for Review.
  • Room rates, walking distance and nightly hotel costs are not verified and are not used in the rankings.
