# Questions, Information & Answers Needed Before Any Decision

These items must be addressed before anyone can reasonably book this trip based on the information we have — and do not have. Each is followed by a suggestion for how to discover the missing information.

## Tournament questions

**Q1. Are the (1Day) Venetian events truly single entry (no re-entry, no add-on)?**
- What we have: Official structure sheets describe ONE starting flight and contain NO re-entry language; multi-flight events explicitly describe re-entry. We interpret (1Day) = single entry.
- What we do NOT have: An explicit "single entry / no re-entry" statement on each (1Day) sheet.
- Needed before deciding: Confirm with the Venetian Poker Room that (1Day) events prohibit re-entry/add-ons.
- How to discover: Call/email the Venetian Poker Room; ask for each event's re-entry policy in writing.

**Q2. What are the guarantees (GTD) for Events 3, 4, 5, 6 (RaceDay Stack and $200 NLH 1Day)?**
- What we have: Buy-in ($200), dates/times. Guarantee not stated in the schedule cell or structure sheet.
- What we do NOT have: GTD value.
- Needed before deciding: Guarantee to judge value.
- How to discover: Venetian Poker Room or the event structure sheet (may list a guarantee elsewhere).

**Q3. Are there other single-entry tournaments on the Strip (Wynn, Aria, Bellagio, MGM Grand, Circa, etc.) in this window?**
- What we have: Leads only from third-party aggregators (PokerAtlas, pokernews.com). Not verified against official room sites.
- What we do NOT have: Official schedules, buy-ins, guarantees, entry rules.
- Needed before deciding: Complete single-entry options across the Strip.
- How to discover: Fetch official poker pages for Wynn (wynnlasvegas.com), Aria (aria.com), Bellagio (bellagio.com), MGM Grand (mgmgrand.com), Circa (circa resort.com); verify against Venetian data.

**Q4. Does the WPT World Championship at Wynn (Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026) have any single-entry side events?**
- What we have: Festival dates; main events are multi-flight re-entry (seen on third-party sites).
- What we do NOT have: Official schedule, buy-ins, entry rules, guarantees.
- Needed before deciding: Whether any WPT side events are single entry and worth adding.
- How to discover: wynnlasvegas.com poker page / WPT official schedule.

**Q5. Can someone really play starting at 8 AM?**
- What we have: Earliest Venetian tournament is 10:10 AM; no 8 AM event exists at this venue.
- What we do NOT have: Any Strip poker room with an  AM single-entry tournament.

- How to discover: Check official schedules of all Strip rooms for early-morning tournaments.

## Hotel questions

**Q6. What is the actual nightly room rate for each hotel for specific dates?**
- What we have: Nothing (rates are dynamic).
- What we do NOT have: Any nightly rate.

- How to discover: Enter exact dates on each hotel's official booking page; record the rate before fees.

**Q7. What is the exact mandatory resort fee per hotel, confirmed on the official site?**
- What we have: Wynn official ($45 or $55 by offer/room). All others from third-party aggregators (conflicting, e.g. ARIA reported as $45 and $55).

**Q9. Is the Venetian/Palazzo worth the likely higher rate vs a cheaper hotel a 15–25 min walk away?**
