## Hotel recommendation
- If distance/convenience is top priority: stay at **The Venetian or Palazzo** (0–2 min walk). You can walk to every tournament.
- If cost/quality is top priority: compute the true all-in cost for Caesars Palace, Paris, The LINQ, Bellagio, ARIA, Wynn/Encore using the formula above, then compare. All are within a 30-minute walk.
- Avoid assuming the advertised nightly rate is what you pay — mandatory resort fees (~$45–$55) and ~13.38% tax can add ~$65–$75+/night.

## What is NOT verified (read before deciding)
- Single-entry status for (1Day) events is a structural interpretation of official structure sheets (one starting flight, no re-entry language). The sheets do not print the words "single entry" or "no re-entry." Flagged for review.
- Hotel nightly rates: not confirmed from any official source (dynamic).
- Resort fees: confirmed only at Wynn via official terms ($45 or $55 by offer). All other resort fees come from third-party aggregators — unverified vs official.
- Other Strip poker rooms (Wynn WPT World Championship, MGM Grand, Aria, Bellagio) were seen only on third-party aggregators and are NOT in the verified tables. See site/flagged-review.md.
- No tournament starts at 8 AM at the Venetian; earliest is 10:10 AM.
