Schedule

Start times are from the official schedule. The earliest verified single-entry start is 10:10 AM. No verified single-entry event starts at 8:00 AM, so a plan that begins at 8:00 AM cannot be built from verified data.

Plan logic

  • Morning anchor: the 10:10 or 11:10 AM events are the earliest single-entry starts in the verified data. Official pages do not publish finish times, so the length of each day depends on how long you last.
  • Evening option: the 5:10 PM bounty events ($200 to $300, verified single entry) are a lower-cost option on the same day. Whether you can play both depends on how long the morning event runs; the official sources do not give that time.
  • Not included: Caesars' 10:00 AM and Wynn's 12:00 PM daily events start earlier than most single-entry events, but they allow re-entry, add-ons or rebuys, so they sit in the advantage section.
