What counts here

  • Re-entry / rebuy: an official rule lets you buy in again after busting.
  • Add-on / paid chips: an official rule lets you buy extra chips, including an optional staff gratuity that buys chips.
  • Buy-in conflict: the official sheet states two different buy-ins, so the buy-in cell is left blank.
