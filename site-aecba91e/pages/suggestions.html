Suggestions — choose a path before implementing

Nothing below is implemented; each is a proposal to improve decision quality. Review, pick the ones worth doing, and they become the next work items.

S1 · Get one written single-entry statement per series

Email the Venetian poker room the list of event numbers and ask for a yes/no on re-entries and add-ons; store the reply as a dated source. This is the only way to move R-F01/Q3/Q4 from 'per sheet' to 'confirmed'.

S2 · Turn the rate log into a nightly capture for the shortlist

Extend the scheduled monitor to store, for each of the 4 candidate hotels, the booking-engine total for your exact dates and occupancy as a dated row, so the site shows a rate trend and the price movement, not one stale quote.

S3 · Add a 'fee waiver / resort credit' column

Resorts World's 'All Resort, No Fees' style offers change true nightly cost by $55+. Without that column the like-for-like comparison is wrong in the user's favour.

S4 · Time the walks once and record the method

One timed walk per pair (start at the lobby desk, end at the poker room door, record date, time, mode) turns eight blank cells into verified rows. Until then the ≤30-min filter is advisory.

S5 · Publish field sizes when results pages populate

Entries per flight turn the guarantee ratio into a real cash-per-entry figure. The DSE 'Results' column is currently blank; capturing it as the series runs is the single highest-value addition.

S6 · Model day-2 equity as a separate row type

Restart rows (#1, #7, #19, #26, #47, #49, #56, #59, #39) are not new entries and currently inflate the row count of a day. A 'continuation' type keeps them visible but out of the entry budget.

S7 · Eligibility filters in the schedule builder

50+ seniors events (#24, #32, #49), women-only (#33, #37, #39) and tag-team (#35) need a player-profile filter, otherwise the plan can include events the player cannot enter.

S8 · Pre-payment re-confirmation gate

Add a checklist that blocks marking any row 'bookable' until the schedule, structure sheet and hotel policy were re-read within 24 hours of payment - the last step of your workflow, currently not enforced by the site.

S9 · One canonical dataset, archive the rest

Twelve per-session dashboards disagree on the single-entry count (0, 20, 30, 36, 45, 64). Move every prior dashboard under archive/ with a banner and keep data/aecba91e as the only input to the site.

S10 · Scenario compare

Store 2-3 trip scenarios (e.g. 'Dec 3-6 $800 flights', 'Dec 12-14 title event', 'cheap $200 grind week') as JSON and render a side-by-side cost/entry comparison from the same data.

S11 · Coverage pass on the other rooms

Re-read Wynn/Bellagio/Caesars/MGM/Golden Nugget/Resorts World with the R1 rule so the single-entry list is not Venetian-only; several of those pages 404'd or were stale in November, which itself should be recorded per venue.

S12 · Surface source-change rows on the day pages

When the monitor flags a changed official page, the affected venue and schedule pages should show the same badge as the tables, so a changed structure sheet cannot be missed while planning a day.
