Executive summary

Verified plan for a Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV trip, published with per-row sources

  • 94 official schedule rows for Nov 16 - Dec 23, 2026 transcribed from the Venetian's own DSE page; all 37 of 37 structure sheets read in full on 2026-11-17, and every published field carries the sheet's own wording.
  • 54 of the 83 dated entry rows qualify as 'single entry per official structure sheet' under the stated rule; 29 rows are multi-flight or fee-discount events, 0 are still unverified because their structure sheet could not be fetched, and the 11 Day 2 / final-table rows are continuations rather than new entries. That is the separation you asked for, and every excluded row says on the page why.
  • Entry cost per confirmed event is known to the dollar: buy-in, the house fee withheld (e.g. $58 of $300 on sheet 5) and the per-entry amount that reaches the prize pool, so the trip calculator multiplies real entry fees, not headline buy-ins.
  • No 8:00 AM start exists in this series. Earliest confirmed starts are 9:10 AM (a $260 satellite on Dec 12) and 10:10 AM (seniors and RaceDay events); the day plans therefore begin at the earliest confirmed start, and that gap is a decision for you (Q5).
  • Hotel side is the weak half of the data: two resort fees are official ($55 + tax at both the Venetian and Wynn, read by earlier sessions), but no property publishes a walking minute figure, parking, cancellation deadline or a dated rate for your dates. The trip calculator therefore takes hotel inputs from the dated rate log and shows the formula rather than a false single number.
  • Change monitoring is live in the repo: a scheduled workflow re-fetches every official URL each morning, records a hash, marks the affected rows 'source changed' and rebuilds the site, so a schedule or fee change cannot be missed between sessions.

What is decided by this data

  • The tournament half is usable: 54 confirmed single-entry events (over 32 dates) with exact buy-in, house fee, per-entry pool, late-registration close and guarantee.
  • The hotel half is not: no official dated room rate, parking, cancellation deadline or walking minute exists in any source read by any session, so no hotel can honestly be ranked.
  • The two open decisions that block the plan are the trip dates and the nightly budget/quality floor (Q1, Q2), plus whether a 9:10/10:10 start is acceptable (Q5).
