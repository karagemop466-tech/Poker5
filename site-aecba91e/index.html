Read this first

  • There is no 8:00 AM start in the official DSE schedule; the earliest confirmed starts are 9:10 AM and 10:10 AM (see Q5).
  • "Single entry" here means: one-day event, its official structure sheet read in full, no re-entry / re-buy / add-on provision printed on it. That is a reading of silence, so the basis is shown in every row (R1).
  • The hotel half of the data is thin: fees are official, but rates, parking, cancellation deadlines and walking minutes are not published by the properties. They are blank and flagged rather than guessed.
