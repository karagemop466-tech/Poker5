Headline findings

  • No Venetian event in this series can be labelled a confirmed freezeout. All 37 structure sheets behind the DeepStack Extravaganza IV schedule were read in full on 2026-11-17; none states single entry, no rebuys or no add-ons, and none caps entries. 51 rows therefore show every other field as verified and the entry rule as not stated; 0 rows carry a confirmed single-entry label. Prior sessions published 31, 30, 20 and 0 for this count (flag F27).
  • Seven multi-flight events are advantage-through-spend by design. Events #01, #07, #19, #26, #47, #49 and #56 each run 2-5 Day 1 flights and state only "Any player who bags chips for Day 2 may not play a subsequent flight" - a player can buy into more than one flight. They live on the multi-entry page, not the ranking (F04).
  • At the Wynn, the opposite is documented: every WPT World Championship event read states re-entry. 11 events state "Unlimited re-entry" until level 9 or 10 and the $1,100 WPT Prime Championship ($5,000,000 GTD) allows one entry plus one re-entry per starting flight, so no WPT event qualifies as single-entry (F36).
  • Guarantee ÷ buy-in is a descriptive ratio, not a forecast, and it is only shown where both numbers are published. Four events (#03, #04, #05, #06) publish no guarantee at all and are not ranked (F39); seven satellites guarantee seats, not cash (F40); bounty guarantees include the bounty pool (F41).
  • Day plan uses official clocks. Overlaps are checked from the scheduled start against each sheet's published late-registration close, not from a guessed duration; event finish times are not published anywhere (F42).
  • Hotel cost is a dated log. The Venetian poker rate ("as low as $119" / $209 Palazzo, conditional on 6 hours of cash play or a $300+ tournament buy-in each night), the $55 + tax resort fee at both the Venetian and Wynn, and the 13.38% Clark County room tax are each cited to an official page with the date read. No machine-readable live rate exists in this environment, so nothing is shown as a current price (F28).
  • No official 8:00 AM start exists. Earliest verified starts: 9:10 AM at the Venetian (event #46, Fri Dec 12), 10:00 AM at the Wynn. The plan is built from the earliest verified start, and the day still runs all day: 10:10 AM / 11:10 AM events are common.
  • Walking routes are blank, not estimated. Only on-property routes (0 minutes) and "the Wynn poker room is located in the Encore tower" are sourced; every other pair has a route link to time it once, which is then reused (F43).
