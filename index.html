Headline findings

  • 31 events are confirmed single-entry from a structure sheet that was read and shows no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or paid-chip text. A further 75 candidate rows remain Unverified because their structure sheet was not read; they are never assumed to be single-entry.
  • Guarantee ÷ entry fee is shown as a descriptive ratio, not a forecast. It describes the published guarantee per dollar of buy-in; it is not a prediction of winnings.
  • Bounty and flight events are labelled separately. Bounty guarantees include bounty payouts; flight events allow more than one entry per flight.
  • Every row carries a "last checked" date. A scheduled job re-reads each official URL and flags any row whose source changed (see the Flagged page).
  • No official 8:00 AM start was found. The earliest verified Venetian start is 9:10 AM. The day plan starts from the earliest verified start.
  • Hotel true nightly cost = room rate + resort fee + tax. Room rates are dynamic; only dated booking-UI snapshots are logged. Walking times are verified only for on-property venues.
