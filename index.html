What changed in this pass

  • 0 events are confirmed single-entry. Silence in a structure sheet does not prove that re-entry, rebuys or add-ons are prohibited. 55 one-day rows have readable sheets but no explicit entry-cap policy, so they are excluded from the single-entry list and flagged for review.
  • Every Venetian structure sheet was read (37 official PDFs), so the rule fields (late-registration cutoff, level length, starting chips, house fee, prize-pool share) are official values, not estimates.
  • Hotel resort fees are now official for The Venetian / The Palazzo ($55), Wynn / Encore ($55), Bellagio ($55), ARIA ($55) and MGM Grand ($50), each with the sentence it was read from. Caesars Palace and Horseshoe fees are still blank.
  • Room tax 13.38% is tied to the Clark County ordinance table for a resort hotel inside the primary gaming corridor.
  • Guarantee conflicts are shown, not resolved. Where the schedule row and the structure sheet print different guarantees, the row says so and links both.

What the data still cannot tell you

  • No 8:00 AM start exists on the Strip. The earliest official start found is 9:00 AM (Horseshoe, six daily events); Caesars Palace opens at 10:00 AM; the Venetian's earliest is 9:10 AM (Dec 12, event #46, a $260 turbo satellite). Among the full tournaments, the Venetian's earliest start is 10:10 AM. The all-day plan therefore starts at 9:00 AM or later, never 8:00 AM.
  • Room rates are dynamic. No official page quotes a fixed rate; the rate log stores dated booking-UI snapshots with their timestamp and nothing else.
  • Walking times between different resorts are not verified. Only on-property routes (0 minutes) pass the 30-minute filter.
  • Finishing times are not published. A playing-day length is derived from each sheet's late-registration level count and is labelled as derived.

The full list, with what would clear each item, is on Questions to resolve and Flagged for review.
