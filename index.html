What this site will not do

  • It will not call an event single-entry unless an official source states the limit.
  • It will not fill a guarantee, a buy-in, a room rate, a resort fee or a walking time that no official source states; those cells stay blank and are listed on the flagged page.
  • It will not rank an event until both its total entry fee and its guarantee are confirmed, and it never turns the guarantee ratio into a forecast.
  • It will not book, pay or store anything: no server, no account, no manual data entry required of you.

Reading order for a decision

1. Can the trip exist at all? Open Day plan — it shows that no official start earlier than 10:10 AM exists in this window, and that a one-day structure with 28 levels runs past midnight, so a second event on the same day is conditional on busting out early.
2. Which events are legal under the policy? Single-entry list and the excluded advantage list.
3. Which is worth playing? Value ranking (guarantee ÷ buy-in, descriptive only).
4. Where to stay and what it really costs? Hotels + Walking routes.
5. Total? Trip cost, then Questions before payment.
