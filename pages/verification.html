The standard a verdict applies (S1)

A Venetian row is published as NO SECOND ENTRY GRANTED (S1 screen) when all of these hold, each with the official excerpt and retrieval date shown on the row: (1) its own structure-sheet PDF was read (HTTP 200, full text, SHA-256 recorded); (2) the sheet names that event number; (3) the row is an entry row, not a Day-2 restart or final table; (4) the sheet has no flight or multi-day structure; (5) the sheet states a registration close; (6) nothing in the sheet's numbered rule list grants a re-entry, re-buy, add-on, buy-back, subsequent/additional/second buy-in, or a purchased or alternate stack. Tests (1)-(6) are re-derivable from the stored fields by scripts/check_ea99006a.py. The screen is deliberately not a confirmation of a freezeout: no sheet in the series states a one-entry maximum in words (flag F01).

What this standard cannot do. No official source in this series states 'one entry only' in words, and every sheet carries 'Management reserves the right to cancel or change the tournament'. Confirmation therefore means 'the published rules grant no second entry', not 'the house promises a freezeout'. That is flag F01 and it stays open. A structure sheet is an announcement, not a contract: every one of them reserves the right for management to change the tournament. A confirmed label therefore means "the published rules grant no second entry on the day they were read", not "the room promises a freezeout next week".

Silence is never promoted to confirmation: a sheet that says nothing about re-entry is published as "cap not stated" and stays out of the ranking, and a sheet that grants a further entry for money moves the row to the multi-entry section, whatever its headline says.
