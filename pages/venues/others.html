Why there is no full schedule for these rooms

  • Caesars Palace publishes a standing daily slate in an editorial page, not a dated calendar; its four daily starts all offer a $50 add-on, so none of them reaches the single-entry list regardless.
  • Horseshoe, Resorts World and South Point publish no dated tournament list that a non-Java client can read; the attempts and their results are on the source monitor.
  • The MGM properties (Bellagio, MGM Grand, ARIA) publish room pages and hotel terms but no retrievable tournament schedule for this window; their hotel terms are still used on the hotels page because they are the official source for the resort fee.
