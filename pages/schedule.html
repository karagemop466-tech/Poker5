Why you can't stack every listed event

Tournaments overlap in time, and many events run across multiple starting flights (Day 1A/1B/1C/1D) plus Day 2 restarts. A realistic one-day plan picks one morning, one early-afternoon, and one evening 1Day event, and confirms none overlap. The recurring 5:10 PM $200 Bounty $8K GTD is the natural evening closer because it starts after the morning/afternoon events finish.

Other days follow the same pattern

Most DSE IV days offer an 10:10–11:10 AM event, a 12:00–12:10 PM event, and a 5:10 PM event. Substitute any date's events using the tournaments page and the official Venetian schedule. Always confirm times and entry rules with the Venetian Poker Room.
