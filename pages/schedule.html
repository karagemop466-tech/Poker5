Day-by-day schedule

How the day plan is built. Each day's primary event is the confirmed single-entry row with the largest published guarantee that day. Its realistic exit is the official start time plus a playing-day length derived from the sheet's late-registration figure (levels x minutes per level + breaks). A fallback is only shown when its official start time is at least 30 minutes after that exit; otherwise the day says "none feasible" rather than proposing a sequence that cannot be played. The derived length is a planning figure, not a published finish time.
8:00 AM cannot be met. No Strip room publishes an 8:00 AM tournament in the sources read. Horseshoe's official daily slate starts at 9:00 AM (six events: 9 AM, 11 AM, 2 PM, 5 PM, 8 PM, 11 PM); Caesars Palace starts at 10:00 AM; the Venetian's earliest is 9:10 AM. A full day is therefore 9:00 AM onward, not 8:00 AM onward.
