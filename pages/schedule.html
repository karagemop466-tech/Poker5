Day plan: one primary event, one conditional fallback

Built from official start times, the late-registration window printed on each structure sheet, and the sheet's own level count and level length. A second event is only offered when the numbers allow it, and when it depends on leaving the first event early the page says so.

The 8 AM question, answered from the schedule

No official source in this window publishes an 8:00 AM start. The earliest recurring Venetian start is 10:10 AM (the RaceDay Stack events) and the usual main-event flight starts at 11:10 AM, with evening flights at 5:10 PM. A day planned from 8:00 AM therefore begins with registration, seating and pre-registration ("all pre-registered stacks will be in play at the start of the event", per the official sheets), not with cards in the air.

60 of 60 venue-days on this page have no event that can start by 8:10 AM. The published level structures also explain why most days hold exactly one event: a 28-level structure at 30 minutes plus three breaks runs about 14 h 45 m, which ends after midnight.

How to read a row

Primary
the day's best published guarantee-per-entry among events that pass the single-entry standard and can start in the morning. If no event on the day has both a buy-in and a guarantee published, the primary is shown with a blank guarantee and the reason says so.
Conditional fallback
a second event whose published registration window is still open while the primary runs. It is only playable if you have already been eliminated — that is the condition, and the time is taken from the fallback's own sheet.
Overlap flags
pairs of events where the later one starts before the earlier one can finish on its published structure. A flagged pair is never both booked.
