Value ranking: guaranteed pool per entry

Ranking is only run on rows where an official source states both the buy-in and the guarantee, because a comparison that mixes a stated guarantee with an unstated one is not a comparison. The number is descriptive: it says how large the posted guarantee is relative to one entry. It is not an expected value, a win probability or a forecast, and field size, which decides both, is not published in advance.

How the ratio is used here

Formula
guaranteed pool ÷ published buy-in. The house fee sits inside the buy-in on these sheets, so no fee is added on top.
Excluded from the ranking
bounty events are ranked in their own block because part of their guarantee is paid by knockout, not by placing; satellites are excluded because their prize is an entry seat; tag-team events are excluded because the entry covers two players; any row where the schedule row and its sheet disagree about the guarantee is excluded until it is re-read.
