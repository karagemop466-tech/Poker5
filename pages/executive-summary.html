What to decide first, in order

  1. Dates, nights and party size. Every cost answer keys off them (Q01).
  2. A nightly cap and a quality standard. No official page publishes a quality score, so the hotel ranking cannot include quality until you pick a standard (Q02, S08).
  3. Whether a named map service may be used as the walking-time source. Until then only on-property hotels can pass the 30-minute rule (Q05, S07).
  4. Whether to read booking rates by hand or link out. The official booking engines are dynamic and restrict automated use (S06).

What this page cannot tell you, and why

  • Room rates: dynamic; the rate log stores dated snapshots instead of a single value, because a single value would be wrong for any other stay.
  • Finishing times: not published anywhere. The exit time on the schedule page is derived from each sheet's late-registration level count and is labelled as derived.
  • Off-property walking times: no casino publishes one; they stay blank.
  • Bellagio, MGM Grand and ARIA tournament schedules: the official material is undated, stale, or absent, so no row from those rooms is classified.
  • Wynn festival rows: the festival window and Main Event dates are official; the per-event structures were not read row by row.
