1. Recommended tournament target

Center the trip on The Venetian Resort's DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026), on the Strip. The standout single-day value is the recurring $200 No Limit Hold'em Bounty $8K GTD (1Day) at 5:10 PM ($200 buy-in vs. $8,000 guarantee). Morning/afternoon 1Day options include the $200 NLH RaceDay Stack (10:10 AM), $200 NLH (11:10 AM, Nov 22), $300 NLH $10K GTD (1Day), $400 PLO Bounty $15K GTD (1Day), and $600 NLH $30K GTD (1Day).

Source: The Venetian Resort — DeepStack Extravaganza IV official schedule

2. Tournaments kept separate (spending advantage)

Multi-flight re-entry events and bounty/add-on events are excluded from the single-entry list and placed on the Re-Entry / Add-On page. These include the $300/$400/$800/$1,100 Day 1A–1D multi-flight events ($75K–$500K GTD), the $2,200 $1M GTD PokerAtlas Championship (Event #47), and the $10,400 WPT World Championship at Wynn (Dec 13–15). In these, buying multiple entries or add-ons can buy an advantage.

Sources: Venetian DSE IV · PokerNews — WPT World Championship

3. Hotel recommendation framework

All recommended hotels are within ~30 minutes' walk of The Venetian. Weight cost and quality higher than distance, but keep distance reasonable.

  • On-site / zero commute: The Venetian Resort — at the tournament, but resort fee is $62.36/night with tax (highest).
  • Closest & budget: Best Western Plus Casino Royale (~4 min walk) — verify whether a resort fee applies.
  • Close mid/budget: Harrah's (~5 min) and Treasure Island (~6 min) — resort fees not yet confirmed.
  • Luxury ~8–13 min walk: Bellagio, Caesars Palace, Wynn, Encore — resort fees $56.69–$62.36/night with tax, before the room rate.

Resort-fee references: unleashvegas.com (2026), lasvegaswonders.com (2026). Walking distances from hotel-listing/mapping sources (approximate).

4. True nightly hotel cost (formula)

True cost/night = room rate + resort fee + 13.38% Clark County lodging tax on the resort fee. Room rates are dynamic and were not confirmed from an official source, so exact true cost per night cannot be stated yet. Resort-fee-only cost (with tax): Venetian/Palazzo/Wynn/Bellagio/Caesars ≈ $56.69–$62.36/night in fees alone.

5. Schedule — 8 AM play-all-day goal

Not achievable as stated. No Strip tournament starts at 8:00 AM. Earliest confirmed Venetian start is ~10:10 AM; Aria's daily starts at 1:00 PM (unverified). A realistic day = one morning event (~10:10–11:10 AM), one early-afternoon event (~12:00–12:10 PM), and the 5:10 PM $200 Bounty $8K GTD closer. See the All-Day Schedule page.

6. Critical open items (must resolve before booking)

  • Confirm single-entry / freezeout status for every 1Day event (structure sheets do not state "no re-entry").
  • Confirm live hotel room rates and whether each hotel charges a resort fee.
  • Confirm the 8 AM start gap — is there any earlier Strip tournament?
  • Confirm Aria and other Strip daily tournament schedules and entry rules (sources conflict/dated).
