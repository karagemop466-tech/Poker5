All 37 sheets behind the Venetian schedule were read in full on 2026-11-17: none states single entry, freezeout, no rebuys or no add-ons, and none caps entries. 51 rows are therefore "sheet read, entry rule not stated" with every other field verified, and the count of confirmed single-entry events is 0. A prior session labelled 31 rows "confirmed" by treating silence as a rule; that label is removed here and flagged (F27).
  3. Seven Venetian events are advantage-through-spend by their own wording. Events #01, #07, #19, #26, #47, #49 and #56 each run 2-5 starting flights and state only that a player who bags chips for Day 2 may not play a later flight - so the same $400-$2,200 can be paid for several Day 1 shots. They are on the multi-entry page (F04).
