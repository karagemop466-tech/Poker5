The answer in six lines

  1. Two series are live for the planned window. The Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV runs 16 Nov – 23 Dec 2026 with “more than $3.6 Million in guaranteed tournament prize pools”, and the WPT World Championship runs 27 Nov – 21 Dec 2026 at Wynn — both read again from the properties' own pages on Thu 19 Nov 2026.
  2. 53 of 247 rows are usable under a single-entry policy. None has an official one-entry statement; 53 have a sheet that was read in full with no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or purchased-chip language, and that is the wording used for them.
  3. 75 rows are excluded and the reason is published for each: starting flights with a separate buy-in, Day 2 restarts, the Wynn Friday rebuy, Caesars' $50 add-on, and two PokerAtlas events whose first buy-in is cheaper for paying members.
  4. An all-day plan from 8 AM is not available anywhere on the Strip in this window: the earliest official start is 10:10 AM, and a 28-level / 30-minute structure runs past midnight, so a day is realistically one event plus a conditional second one only if you bust early.
  5. Hotel: the host property wins on the two things that can be verified. The Venetian / Palazzo is the only hotel with a 0-minute indoor route to the poker room, a $55 + tax mandatory fee stated on its own page, $20/day parking that is free to a player with 3 h+ of play, and an official poker-suite rate starting at $119.
  6. The one thing still missing is a dated room rate. Every official booking engine needs JavaScript, so the trip total stays Incomplete until a checkout quote is logged — the alternative would be publishing a number nobody stated.

Top risks, each with its page

  • Silence is not a rule. Grade B means the sheet says nothing, not that re-entry is forbidden. See the standard.
  • Guarantees are not fields. A $15K guarantee with 40 entrants is $375 per entry, less than the buy-in; the ratio on this site is descriptive only. See Value ranking.
  • The resort fee is taxed. (rate + fee) × 1.1338, not rate × 1.1338 + fee. See Hotels.
  • Hotel quality has no official measure, so the brief's "quality over distance" preference cannot be scored here; see flagged item B05.
  • The published schedule moves. Rows carry a last-checked date and a change badge; treat anything older than a week as unconfirmed. See Source monitor.
