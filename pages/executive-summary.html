What to do before deciding

  1. Request written event-specific maximum-entry/no-rebuy/no-add-on terms before treating S1 as a freezeout guarantee.
  2. Choose actual dates, occupancy, budget and a hotel quality floor; current controls are scenario defaults, not traveler preferences.
  3. Obtain an official booking checkout total for that same stay, including room charge, mandatory resort fee, tax, parking and cancellation deadline.
  4. Plan one event per day first. Add a conditional second only when actual event duration leaves a realistic exit and registration window.
  5. Reconfirm schedules, structure sheets, room policies and hotel terms before non-refundable payment.
