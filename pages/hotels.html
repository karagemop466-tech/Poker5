Hotels: true cost per night

True nightly cost = room rate + resort fee + tax. The tax rate below is taken from the Clark County ordinance; resort fees are stated only where an official page publishes them. Room rates are not filled in - official booking engines are JavaScript applications and no dated rate could be read from an official source. Use the booking link, then record what you see in the dated log at the bottom of this page.

The budget and quality filters only hide rows that are known to miss your ceiling or floor. Rows without a dated quote are never hidden by the budget filter - a missing rate is not a cheap rate.
