Hotels Within Walking Distance of the Tournaments

Tournament venue: The Venetian Resort (Las Vegas Strip). All hotels below are within a ~30-minute walk. Cost and quality are weighted higher than distance, but distance stays reasonable.

Venetian official site →

How "true cost per night" is built

True cost/night = room rate + mandatory resort fee + 13.38% Clark County lodging tax on the resort fee. Resort fees are mandatory per-room/per-night and are taxed. Room rates are dynamic and could not be confirmed from an official source, so they are left blank/unverified — check each hotel's official site (links provided) for current rates before booking.

Unverified hotel values

  • Nightly room rates are dynamic and were not confirmed from an official source. All are marked unverified; use the official booking links above for live rates.
  • Resort fees for Harrah's, Treasure Island, Casino Royale, and LINQ were not confirmed; marked "Not confirmed." Strip-wide 2026 sources place most Strip resort fees at $45–$55 pre-tax ($51–$62 with tax).
  • Resort-fee figures for Venetian/Palazzo/Wynn/Bellagio/Caesars come from 2026 secondary sources (unleashvegas.com, lasvegaswonders.com) and show some variance between sources ($50 vs $55 for Venetian/Palazzo/Wynn). Confirm on each hotel's official site.
  • Walking distances come from hotel-listing/mapping sources and are approximate; confirm on a map.
  • Quality tiers are subjective and unverified.

Suggested priority (subject to live-rate check)

  • Best value / closest: Best Western Plus Casino Royale (~4 min walk, budget) — verify whether a resort fee applies.
  • On-site convenience: The Venetian Resort (0 min, at the tournament) — highest convenience, highest resort fee ($62.36/night with tax).
  • Luxury within ~12 min walk: Wynn/Encore or Bellagio — higher quality, $56.69–$62.36/night in fees alone before the room rate.
