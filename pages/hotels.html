Hotels & true nightly cost

True cost of a hotel night = room rate + mandatory resort fee + tax on both. The room rate is the only part that moves daily, and no official page publishes a fixed rate, so it is kept as a dated log rather than a single number. The resort fee and the tax rate below are official: the fee from each hotel's own page, the 13.38% tax from the Clark County ordinance table for a resort hotel inside the primary gaming corridor (Resort hotel within primary gaming corridor · 12.5% · 0.88% · 13.38%).
