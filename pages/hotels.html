Hotels: Rates, True Cost and Walking

How the true nightly cost is built: nightly room rate + mandatory resort fee + tax. Room rates are dynamic and no official machine-readable rate feed exists, so nothing here is a "current rate": the dated log below stores each observation with the date and source it came from, and the hotels table shows the official "as low as" poker rate only with its conditions. Tax: 13.38% for a resort hotel inside the Clark County Primary Gaming Corridor, cited from the official ordinance document; whether the resort fee sits inside the tax base is confirmed only on the itemised folio (F45).
