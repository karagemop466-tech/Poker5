Hotels: true cost per night

True nightly cost = room rate + resort fee + tax. The tax rate below is taken from the Clark County ordinance; resort fees are stated only where an official page publishes them - all five candidate hotels now have one. Room rates are filled in only from dated captures: the Wynn booking engine published dated quotes for 2026-12-06 to 2026-12-13, and the Horseshoe and Resorts World engines published December calendars; the Venetian engine returned "$XXX" placeholders and the Caesars Palace calendar errored on 2026-11-19. Use the booking link to complete the picture, then record what you see in the dated log at the bottom of this page.

The budget and quality filters only hide rows that are known to miss your ceiling or floor. Rows without a dated quote are never hidden by the budget filter - a missing rate is not a cheap rate.

Tax note: Clark County room tax: 13.38% (official ordinance). The Venetian hotel terms confirm it from the hotel side: 'Daily rates do not include the applicable Clark County Room Tax of 13.38%' (read 2026-11-19). The Horseshoe fee tax ($6.68 on $49.95) and Caesars Palace fee tax ($7.35 on $54.95) are consistent with it. The Wynn quotes display 'Includes Resort Fee, Excludes taxes' and returned no tax figure.
