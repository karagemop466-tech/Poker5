Hotels: the true nightly cost, itemised

Cost and quality outrank distance, but the walk still has to be under 30 minutes. This page publishes every cost component an official page states — mandatory resort fee, room tax, parking, deposit and cancellation deadline — and leaves the room rate blank until a dated quote is logged, because the official booking engines will not give one to a non-Java client.

The formula and its two sources

True cost per night
(room rate + mandatory resort fee) × (1 + 13.38%). The multiplier is the Clark County combined transient lodging tax for a resort hotel inside the primary gaming corridor: 12.5% + 0.88% stadium-district tax, re-read Thu 19 Nov 2026.
Why the fee is taxed too
the same ordinance defines “rent” to include separately stated accommodation, facility and access fees, so a mandatory resort fee is part of the tax base.
Compared like-for-like
only a checkout total for the same dates, same occupancy and same night count can be divided by nights and compared; that is what the dated log below is for.

Quality, honestly

No quality floor is applied, because no official page publishes a comparable hotel quality rating and copying star ratings or review scores would break the official-source rule. Hotels are ordered by true nightly cost, mandatory-fee transparency, parking and verified walk time instead, and every row keeps its own source link.
