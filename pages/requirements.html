Requirements: what was asked, what is on the site, what is blocked

Line-by-line traceability for the project brief: every requirement in the request, the status on the published site, where the control or table lives, and the evidence or the reason it cannot be completed. Statuses: 'implemented' (the control and its data exist), 'implemented, limit published' (built, with the honest boundary printed next to it), 'blocked on an official source' (the site refuses to fill it from anything but the property's own page - see the linked flag). Written by session 0fd4c550 on 2026-11-19 after re-reading the published pages and the official sources.

Each chip filters this table to that status.

What each status means

implemented
The requirement is met by a control or a table on the published site.
implemented, limit published
Built, and the boundary of what the data can prove is printed next to it rather than hidden.
blocked on an official source
The site will not invent the value: no official page states it, so the cell stays blank and the reason is flagged.

The rows marked blocked on an official source are not unfinished work in the ordinary sense: they are the places where the brief asks for a number the properties do not publish - an expected finish time, a walking time between two hotels, a dated room rate for the stay. Filling those cells would mean inventing them, so the cell stays blank, the flag stays open, and the maps or booking link that settles the question in sixty seconds is printed beside it.
