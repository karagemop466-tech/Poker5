What a reader should conclude

  • The Venetian and the Palazzo are one connected complex. The poker room is officially inside The Palazzo on level 2 of the Grand Canal Shoppes, so a room in either tower is an indoor, elevator-connected walk with no street crossing — the only route this build can verify as well inside 30 minutes, and the reason the host-property hotel is the default.
  • Resorts World, Caesars Palace, Horseshoe and Wynn are all within a plausible Strip walk, but no official page states the minutes, so the numbers are blank here and the map links are provided instead. Summer heat, construction detours and the indoor routes are not in anyone's published number.
  • The 30-minute rule is applied by excluding unverified rows from the plan, not by guessing a time that would pass it. Every row that stays in the plan is a verified on-property walk.
