How a row earns the "Confirmed single-entry" badge. The structure sheet linked in the row was downloaded from the official Venetian site, its own list of event numbers includes the row's event number, the sheet describes a one-day event, and the text contains no re-entry, rebuy, add-on, double-entry, alternate-stack or purchased-chip language. The row shows the sheet's own words for the late-registration cutoff, level length, starting chips and house fee, and the date the sheet was read. A sheet that is silent on re-entry is the published confirmation standard; it is stated on this page and on Sources & method so you can judge it. Rows that fail any part of that test are on Flagged for review, not here.
