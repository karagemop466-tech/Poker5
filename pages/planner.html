Trip Cost Planner

Trip cost calculator. Estimated total = (entries × buy-ins) + (nights × true hotel cost per night), where true hotel cost = dated room rate + mandatory resort fee + tax. No date-specific room quote is currently recorded, so the hotel and grand totals stay incomplete until a rate is supplied; missing inputs are never treated as zero. Every buy-in comes from the linked official schedule. Event options show their entry-policy status and do not imply single-entry when the source is silent. This is a planning estimate, not a booking quote.
