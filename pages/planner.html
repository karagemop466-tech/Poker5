Trip cost: entries × buy-ins + nights × true hotel cost

Estimated trip cost = one listed buy-in per selected event + nights × (room rate + mandatory resort fee) × (1 + room tax) + parking where it applies. Nothing here needs to be typed. The window, the hotel's mandatory fee and tax, and every buy-in come from the same official rows as the rest of the site; the only optional input is a dated room quote you choose to log. Until that exists the calculator refuses a grand total and prints Incomplete rather than inventing one.

Scenario

The dates and the default hotel above are an automatically selected research scenario, not confirmed travel dates, and the walk to the poker room is not verified for every hotel (see Walking routes).

The mode selector “official published rate floor” uses only a number the property itself publishes (for the Venetian: $119 + $55, taxed at 13.38%) and labels the result an estimate. It is not a quote for these dates: the page says rates start as low as that figure, are subject to qualifying play and availability, and revert to the prevailing rate for any night the play condition is not met.

What the total does and does not include

Included
one listed buy-in per selected event; nights × (room rate + mandatory resort fee) × 1.1338 where a rate basis exists.
Deliberately not included
re-entry or add-on money (the events offering them are not in this list), travel, food, any fee a hotel adds at the desk that is not stated on an official page, and airfare to or from Las Vegas.
Why no event-count multiplier
an earlier version had a "re-entry multiplier" box, which made a single-entry trip look like it had a cheap way to add attempts. If you choose to re-enter, you are outside the single-entry policy: model that on the advantage page instead.
Cancellation exposure
the Wynn terms read today require a one-night room + tax deposit and a 72-hour cancellation deadline; the Venetian and Caesars pages read today do not state a single corporate deadline, so it stays blank rather than assumed.
