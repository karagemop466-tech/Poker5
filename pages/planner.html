Trip cost calculator

Estimated trip cost = entries × buy-ins + nights × true hotel cost per night, where true hotel cost = room rate + mandatory resort fee + tax. Every buy-in and fee in the picker comes from the official source linked on these pages. The room rate is the one number you have to read off the official booking page for your dates; nothing here guesses it. This is a planning estimate, not a booking quote.

1. Stay

Fee is loaded from the hotel you pick and confirmed on that hotel's official page. Tax is the Clark County rate tied to the ordinance table. An unknown rate is never treated as zero: the trip total stays incomplete until a dated room quote is entered.

The 13.38% figure is the Clark County ordinance rate for a resort hotel inside the primary gaming corridor. Whether the resort fee is included in the tax base remains unverified on the official pages read; the fee's own tax treatment is confirmed on the folio at checkout.
