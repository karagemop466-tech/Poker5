Questions, answers and what is still missing

21 questions that have to be answered before a decision is reasonable. For each: what the verified data already answers, what is still missing, and how to close the gap.

Q01. What are your exact trip dates, your nightly budget ceiling and your minimum hotel quality?

What we have
A planning scenario is fixed automatically: check-in 2026-12-06, check-out 2026-12-13 (7 nights), chosen on 2026-11-19 from the corrected single-entry set (12 entries, about $335,000 of published guarantees). The check-out date is the morning after the last playing day. The displayed nightly budget ceiling is the average nightly room charge from one dated booking observation that is NOT for this stay, and the quality floor is a property-tier label, not an independent rating; both are explained on the Hotels and Trip planner pages. No input is required to read the plan.
What is missing
The traveller's own dates, budget ceiling and minimum quality standard are still unknown. The scenario above is the site's automatic choice, not your decision, and no hotel has a dated, itemized quote for it, so the trip total stays 'Incomplete'.
How to find it
Read the scenario as a starting point, then obtain official itemized quotes for the same nights, occupancy and room class before treating any hotel figure as a comparison. The date filter on every table re-scopes every row.

Q02. Is any DSE IV event actually a freezeout?

What we have
All 37 official structure sheets were read. Under S1, 53 rows are classified 'Confirmed single-entry (standard S1)' because their own sheet contains no rule granting another entry; none of the sheets explicitly promises a one-entry maximum or uses 'freezeout' as a guarantee.
What is missing
A written, event-number-specific statement from the Venetian that the entry limit is one, with no re-entry, rebuy, add-on, or other paid advantage. The S1 label describes the published terms found; it is not the venue's explicit promise.
How to find it
Ask the Venetian tournament desk for written maximum-entry/no-rebuy/no-add-on terms for each candidate event number. Until then, treat S1 as a rules-based screen rather than a guaranteed freezeout and keep the limitation visible.

Q03. What is the true nightly hotel cost for your dates?

What we have
Resort fees: Venetian $55 + tax (official), Resorts World $55 per room per day (official, waived on the 'All Resort, No Fees' offer through 28 Dec 2026). Tax: 13.38% (Clark County ordinance).
What is missing
The nightly room rate itself, parking charges, and each hotel's cancellation deadline for your dates.
How to find it
Open the official booking link on the Hotels page for your dates, read the itemised total on the checkout screen (room, resort fee, tax, parking) and paste the figures into the rate log. The log keeps each entry dated and computes true cost/night = rate + resort fee + tax.

Q04. Is the walk from your hotel to the poker room under 30 minutes?

What we have

What is missing

How to find it

Q05. Can two events on the same day actually be played back to back?

Q12. Is the mandatory resort fee inside the taxed base on the final folio?

What we have
The Clark County ordinance defines 'rent' to include mandatory charges; both Venetian and Wynn state the fee is 'plus tax'. The site's calculator therefore taxes rate + fee and says so.
What is missing
An itemised checkout folio that shows exactly what was taxed at which rate for the booked rate plan.
How to find it
Compare the booking page's estimated total with the checkout folio on one stay; record both in the dated rate log.

Q14. What is each candidate hotel's cancellation deadline and deposit policy for the planned rate?

What we have
Official hotel reservation terms now establish a standard cancellation deadline for four properties, re-read 2026-11-19: Wynn/Encore 48 hours before arrival for offer DK26WLV2 (one night's deposit forfeited); Bellagio, ARIA and MGM Grand 72 hours before arrival for the standard reservation (first night's deposit forfeited). Resorts World requires 48 hours' notice for a deposit refund under its published offer. Caesars Palace and Horseshoe advertise 'FREE 48-Hour Cancellation' on the booking widget but no term sheet has been read for a specific rate plan.
What is missing
The deadline that applies to the exact rate plan you book: every MGM page says 'unless specifically excluded by booked offer', Wynn's figure belongs to a two-night promotional offer, and Caesars/Horseshoe publish the headline but not the term sheet.
How to find it
Read the cancellation paragraph on the confirmation page for the chosen rate before paying any deposit, and record it in the dated rate log. Any rate that cannot be cancelled for free is a risk against the whole trip budget.

Q17. How long will you play each event, and therefore which two events can actually be played in sequence on the same day?

What we have
Official start times and late-registration closes for every Venetian and Wynn row (structure sheets). The day schedule now tests sequencing for any playing length you enter (0.5 to 12 hours), and it shows which pairs cannot be played in sequence at that length.
What is missing
No official source publishes a finishing time or a typical playing length for any of these events, so the check depends on an estimate that only you can supply.
How to find it
Enter your own estimate in the 'Assumed playing length' box on the Day schedule page. It is never written into the data or shown as an official value. To remove the estimate, find a published finish time or a level-by-level structure sheet that implies one, and record its source.

Q20. What would a day actually look like from 8 AM, and how many events can really be played in one day?
