Questions, answers and what is still missing

19 questions that have to be answered before a decision is reasonable. For each: what the verified data already answers, what is still missing, and how to close the gap.

Q01. What are your exact trip dates, your nightly budget ceiling and your minimum hotel quality?

What we have
The dashboard uses an automatic scenario: 2026-12-06 to 2026-12-13 (7 nights), selected from the captured schedule on 2026-11-19. The date choice is not traveler-provided. The budget ceiling is based on a dated, unverified booking observation, and the Luxury quality floor is a property-tier label, not an independent quality rating.
What is missing
The traveler’s preferred dates, budget ceiling, occupancy, and minimum quality standard are unknown. The automatic settings are scenario defaults only; the dated hotel quote used to derive the ceiling is not for the selected stay and is not a verified current rate.
How to find it
Treat the Dates and Budget controls on the Hotels/Planner pages as scenario inputs. Replace them with the actual trip window, occupancy, budget, and chosen quality floor before deciding; then obtain a date-matched official itemized hotel quote.

Q02. Is any DSE IV event actually a freezeout?

What we have
All 37 official structure sheets were read. Under S1, 53 rows are classified 'Confirmed single-entry (standard S1)' because their own sheet contains no rule granting another entry; none of the sheets explicitly promises a one-entry maximum or uses 'freezeout' as a guarantee.
What is missing
A written, event-number-specific statement from the Venetian that the entry limit is one, with no re-entry, rebuy, add-on, or other paid advantage. The S1 label describes the published terms found; it is not the venue's explicit promise.
How to find it
Ask the Venetian tournament desk for written maximum-entry/no-rebuy/no-add-on terms for each candidate event number. Until then, treat S1 as a rules-based screen rather than a guaranteed freezeout and keep the limitation visible.

Q03. What is the true nightly hotel cost for your dates?

What we have
Resort fees (all five candidates, official): Venetian $55 plus tax; Wynn $55 plus tax; Caesars Palace $62.30 total ($54.95 plus $7.35 tax); Horseshoe $56.63 total ($49.95 plus $6.68 tax); Resorts World $55 waived on the 'All Resort, No Fees' offer through 28 Dec 2026. Tax 13.38% (Clark County ordinance, confirmed by the Venetian hotel terms). Dated Wynn quotes for Dec 6-13: $688-$818 average per night (fee included, taxes excluded). Dated December calendars for Horseshoe and Resorts World (raw displays). Parking and cancellation terms for all five (official).
What is missing
An itemised checkout total for the actual stay at any hotel (room + fee + tax + parking): the Wynn pages show averages only, the calendars are raw daily displays with an undefined per-date meaning, and the Venetian and Caesars Palace engines returned no rates at all on 2026-11-19.
How to find it
Open the official booking link on the Hotels page for your dates and read the itemised checkout screen; record it in the dated log. The Wynn calculator figure can already be entered from the captured quote ($688-$818/night); the rest need a checkout total first.

Q04. Is the walk from your hotel to the poker room under 30 minutes?

What we have
On-property pairs are 0 minutes and verified from official pages.
What is missing
Every cross-property walking time. No official source publishes one.
How to find it
Open the route link on the Routes page (Google Maps walking directions) and time it yourself, or ask the hotel. The 30-minute filter works on what you record.

Q05. Can two events on the same day actually be played back to back?

What we have
Official start times, official level lengths, official late-registration close times, and the rule that each Day 1 plays down to about 12.5% of the field.
What is missing
Finish times. No official source states when an event ends.
How to find it
The schedule page gives each day a primary event and a conditional fallback, and marks overlap using the structure clock rather than a guessed finish time. Confirm the day's plan at the desk on the morning.

Q06. Do the Wynn daily tournaments run during the WPT World Championship?

What we have
The official page says dailies are suspended during Wynn Signature, Wynn Millions and Classic series.
What is missing
Whether the WPT World Championship festival (27 Nov - 21 Dec 2026) also suspends them.
How to find it
Call the Wynn poker room on (702) 770-7654 (printed on the official schedule PDF) or check wynnpoker.com.

Q08. What is the entry fee split and the real cost of an entry?

What we have
House fee and prize-pool contribution for every Venetian event, straight from each structure sheet.
What is missing
The equivalent split for WPT events not individually read, and for the Caesars daily.
How to find it
Read the 'Entry Fee' line on the official structure sheet for the event. The Tournaments page shows the split where it was published.

Q09. How many entries should the trip budget for?

What we have
Buy-in for every verified row, plus a calculator for entries x buy-ins + nights x true hotel cost.
What is missing
Your bankroll and your appetite for re-entry (which multiplies the cost of a multi-entry event).
How to find it
Set the number of entries per event on the Trip planner page; it prices single-entry and multi-entry plans side by side.

Q10. Are the guarantees in this table comparable to each other?

What we have
Each guarantee exactly as the official sheet states it, including whether bounties are inside it.
What is missing
A like-for-like comparison where one room counts bounties in the guarantee and another does not.
How to find it
The ratio column (guarantee / entry fee) is labelled descriptive, not a forecast, and bounty and flight events are labelled separately. Compare only within a format.

Q11. What will parking add to the stay, and is the poker-room validation usable for the planned schedule?

What we have
Venetian: complimentary self-parking for players with 3+ hours of tournament or cash play (official player-parking page). Wynn: complimentary self-parking for registered guests and poker-room guests; non-guest and valet prices published.
What is missing
Whether the planned nights qualify for validation each day, event-day parking rates, and Caesars/MGM parking terms for the candidate hotels.
How to find it
Ask the poker room at registration; read each hotel's official parking page before booking.

Q12. Is the mandatory resort fee inside the taxed base on the final folio?

What we have
The Clark County ordinance defines 'rent' to include mandatory charges; both Venetian and Wynn state the fee is 'plus tax'. The site's calculator therefore taxes rate + fee and says so.
What is missing
An itemised checkout folio that shows exactly what was taxed at which rate for the booked rate plan.
How to find it
Compare the booking page's estimated total with the checkout folio on one stay; record both in the dated rate log.

Q13. Do Caesars Palace and Horseshoe publish their daily tournament buy-ins, guarantees and entry rules anywhere official?

What we have
The official Caesars page lists four Caesars Palace starts and six Horseshoe starts; the Caesars Palace daily flyer does not state entry rules.
What is missing
Published buy-ins/guarantees for the Horseshoe dailies and a written entry rule for the Caesars dailies.
How to find it
Ask the Caesars Palace and Horseshoe poker rooms for the current daily structure sheet; store it with a retrieval date.

Q14. What is each candidate hotel's cancellation deadline and deposit policy for the planned rate?

What we have
Cancellation terms (official, read 2026-11-19): Venetian - first-night or full-stay deposit at confirmation, prepaid offers non-refundable, no-show forfeits the deposit, early-departure fee $100 plus tax. Wynn - one night's room rate and tax at booking; 72-hour free cancellation on Flexible Getaway / Fall offers, 48-hour on DK26WLV2 and Wynn Rewards Exclusive. Caesars Palace - FREE 48-Hour Cancellation, deposit $100/day. Horseshoe - 48-hour, deposit $50/day. Resorts World - first-night deposit, 48-hour free cancellation, $150/night incidentals.
What is missing
Which exact rate plan will be booked at each hotel (cancellation terms are rate-plan-specific at the Wynn), and the cancellation terms behind the displayed Horseshoe/Resorts World calendar figures.
How to find it
Read the rate rules shown on each official booking page at quote time and record them in the dated rate log.

Q15. Will the morning re-check see a schedule change in every Strip room, or only where a page exists?

What we have
The monitor re-reads every official URL that backs a row (Venetian schedule + 37 sheets, Wynn/WPT PDFs, Caesars page, South Point pages, MGM pages) every morning and records per-room status on the Source monitor page.
What is missing
Official daily-schedule pages for rooms that publish only images (South Point) or nothing (Horseshoe, Resorts World); a guarantee that a PDF re-host does not look like a rule change.
How to find it
Treat the per-room feed as presence-and-change detection, not as content proof; a 'changed' row means a human should re-read the source before relying on the table.

Q16. Is the calculator's hotel input backed by a dated quote for these dates?

What we have
For the Wynn yes: $688 ('Your Fall to Have It All') and $818 (Flexible Getaway) average per night for Dec 6-13, 2026, fee included, taxes excluded (official booking engine, read 2026-11-19). The Venetian $119/$209 poker-suite rates are floors only.

Itemised checkout totals for the Venetian, Caesars Palace, Horseshoe and Resorts World; the calculator stays incomplete until one is recorded for the hotel actually chosen.

Q17. How long will you play each event, and therefore which two events can actually be played in sequence on the same day?

What we have
Official start times and late-registration closes for every Venetian and Wynn row (structure sheets). The day schedule now tests sequencing for any playing length you enter (0.5 to 12 hours), and it shows which pairs cannot be played in sequence at that length.
What is missing
No official source publishes a finishing time or a typical playing length for any of these events, so the check depends on an estimate that only you can supply.
How to find it
Enter your own estimate in the 'Assumed playing length' box on the Day schedule page. It is never written into the data or shown as an official value. To remove the estimate, find a published finish time or a level-by-level structure sheet that implies one, and record its source.

Q18. Which entrance does each walking route use, and should the limit be judged per direction?

What we have
Google Maps walking times measured both ways on 2026-11-19 are stored per direction (e.g. Wynn to Venetian 14 min vs Venetian to Wynn 26 min; Horseshoe to Venetian 31 min vs the other way 30 min).
What is missing
The official entrance each route targets, and a rule for which direction the 30-minute limit applies to. Both directions are shown so the reader can decide.
How to find it
Open the route link per row and walk it once at the pace you would actually use, from the entrance you would leave by; record your own minutes in the panel on the routes page.

Q19. What does a date in the Caesars/Horseshoe/Resorts World calendars price - the night of that date, or a stay starting it?

What we have
The raw December 2026 displays for each candidate hotel (stored per date in the rate log and stay captures). The Horseshoe caption: 'Rates shown reflect the lowest available rate and include applicable resort fees. All pricing may vary by length of stay.'
What is missing
The per-date meaning; the pages do not state it. Until it is confirmed, the figures are not added up into a stay total and no nightly average is claimed.
How to find it
Ask the hotel's reservations line in writing, or run a one-night and a multi-night query for the same date and compare what the engine displays.
