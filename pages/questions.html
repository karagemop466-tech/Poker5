Questions, answers and what is still missing

18 questions that have to be answered before a decision is reasonable. For each: what the verified data already answers, what is still missing, and how to close the gap.

Q01. What are your exact trip dates, your nightly budget ceiling and your minimum hotel quality?

What we have
The dashboard uses an automatic scenario: 2026-12-06 to 2026-12-13 (7 nights), selected from the captured schedule on 2026-11-19 (12 full-field entries, about $335,000 of published guarantees). The date choice is not traveler-provided. The budget ceiling is based on a dated, unverified booking observation, and the Luxury quality floor is a property-tier label, not an independent quality rating.
What is missing
The traveler’s preferred dates, budget ceiling, occupancy, and minimum quality standard are unknown. The automatic settings are scenario defaults only; the dated hotel quote used to derive the ceiling is not for the selected stay and is not a verified current rate.
How to find it
Treat the Dates and Budget controls on the Hotels/Planner pages as scenario inputs. Replace them with the actual trip window, occupancy, budget, and chosen quality floor before deciding; then obtain a date-matched official itemized hotel quote.

Q02. Is any DSE IV event actually a freezeout?

What we have
All 37 official structure sheets were read. Under S1, 53 rows are classified 'Confirmed single-entry (standard S1)' because their own sheet contains no rule granting another entry; none of the sheets explicitly promises a one-entry maximum or uses 'freezeout' as a guarantee.
What is missing
A written, event-number-specific statement from the Venetian that the entry limit is one, with no re-entry, rebuy, add-on, or other paid advantage. The S1 label describes the published terms found; it is not the venue's explicit promise.
How to find it
Ask the Venetian tournament desk for written maximum-entry/no-rebuy/no-add-on terms for each candidate event number. Until then, treat S1 as a rules-based screen rather than a guaranteed freezeout and keep the limitation visible.

Q03. What is the true nightly hotel cost for your dates?

What we have
Resort fees: Venetian $55 + tax (official), Resorts World $55 per room per day (official, waived on the 'All Resort, No Fees' offer through 28 Dec 2026). Tax: 13.38% (Clark County ordinance).
What is missing
The nightly room rate itself, parking charges, and each hotel's cancellation deadline for your dates.
How to find it
Open the official booking link on the Hotels page for your dates, read the itemised total on the checkout screen (room, resort fee, tax, parking) and paste the figures into the rate log. The log keeps each entry dated and computes true cost/night = rate + resort fee + tax.

Q04. Is the walk from your hotel to the poker room under 30 minutes?

What we have
On-property pairs are 0 minutes and verified from official pages.
What is missing
Every cross-property walking time. No official source publishes one.
How to find it
Open the route link on the Routes page (Google Maps walking directions) and time it yourself, or ask the hotel. The 30-minute filter works on what you record.

Q05. Can two events on the same day actually be played back to back?

What we have
Official start times, official level lengths, official late-registration close times, and the rule that each Day 1 plays down to about 12.5% of the field.
What is missing
Finish times. No official source states when an event ends.
How to find it
The schedule page gives each day a primary event and a conditional fallback, and marks overlap using the structure clock rather than a guessed finish time. Confirm the day's plan at the desk on the morning.

Q06. Do the Wynn daily tournaments run during the WPT World Championship?

What is missing
Whether the WPT World Championship festival (27 Nov - 21 Dec 2026) also suspends them.
How to find it
Call the Wynn poker room on (702) 770-7654 (printed on the official schedule PDF) or check wynnpoker.com.

Buy-in for every verified row, plus a calculator for entries x buy-ins + nights x true hotel cost.

Set the number of entries per event on the Trip planner page; it prices single-entry and multi-entry plans side by side.

Q10. Are the guarantees in this table comparable to each other?

What we have
Each guarantee exactly as the official sheet states it, including whether bounties are inside it.
What is missing
A like-for-like comparison where one room counts bounties in the guarantee and another does not.
How to find it
The ratio column (guarantee / entry fee) is labelled descriptive, not a forecast, and bounty and flight events are labelled separately. Compare only within a format.

Q11. What will parking add to the stay, and is the poker-room validation usable for the planned schedule?

What we have
Venetian (official self-parking page, re-read 2026-11-19): registered hotel guests pay $20 per day, seven days a week; valet is $40 per day; non-hotel guests pay $20 Mon-Thu and $25 Fri-Sun; and poker-room players with three hours or more of tournament or cash play get that day's self-parking free after a supervisor confirms the session. Wynn: complimentary self-parking for up to two vehicles is included in the $55 resort fee, and separate Wynn terms extend complimentary self-parking to poker-room guests.
What is missing
Whether a tournament day of play reliably reaches the Venetian's three-hour validation window, whether event parking rates are in effect for the planned dates, and Caesars/MGM parking terms for the candidate hotels (their official pages do not state a guest rate here).
How to find it
Ask the poker room at registration about daily validation, and read the parking page in the booking flow before paying; the $20/night figure is a property-wide rate, not a per-stay quote.

Q12. Is the mandatory resort fee inside the taxed base on the final folio?

What we have
The Clark County ordinance defines 'rent' to include mandatory charges; both Venetian and Wynn state the fee is 'plus tax'. The site's calculator therefore taxes rate + fee and says so.
What is missing
An itemised checkout folio that shows exactly what was taxed at which rate for the booked rate plan.
How to find it
Compare the booking page's estimated total with the checkout folio on one stay; record both in the dated rate log.

Q13. Do Caesars Palace and Horseshoe publish their daily tournament buy-ins, guarantees and entry rules anywhere official?

What we have
The official Caesars page lists four Caesars Palace starts and six Horseshoe starts; the Caesars Palace daily flyer does not state entry rules.
What is missing
Published buy-ins/guarantees for the Horseshoe dailies and a written entry rule for the Caesars dailies.
How to find it
Ask the Caesars Palace and Horseshoe poker rooms for the current daily structure sheet; store it with a retrieval date.

Q14. What is each candidate hotel's cancellation deadline and deposit policy for the planned rate?

What we have
Venetian (official pages, read 2026-11-19): the published refundable 'Most Flexible Rate' gives a full refund if cancelled no later than 24 hours prior to arrival, with a deposit of the first night (suite, fee and tax) billed at confirmation; property-wide hotel terms add that prepaid offers are non-cancellable and non-refundable, that check-in is 3:00 p.m.-11:59 p.m. and check-out is 11:00 a.m., and that a $150 per night incidental card hold applies to non-Signature suites. Wynn offer terms DK26WLV2, re-read 2026-11-19: cancellations must be received at least 48 hours before arrival for that offer's bookings; the earlier 72-hour figure is withdrawn (F55).
What is missing
The cancellation deadline for the Venetian poker suite rate and for the rate actually quoted at checkout (the offer pages publish their own deadlines, which differ), and any Caesars or MGM property-specific deadline.
How to find it
Read the rate rules shown on the official booking page at quote time and record them in the dated rate log before any non-refundable payment.

Q15. Will the morning re-check see a schedule change in every Strip room, or only where a page exists?

What we have
The monitor re-reads every official URL that backs a row (Venetian schedule + 37 sheets, Wynn/WPT PDFs, Caesars page, South Point pages, MGM pages) every morning and records per-room status on the Source monitor page.
What is missing
Official daily-schedule pages for rooms that publish only images (South Point) or nothing (Horseshoe, Resorts World); a guarantee that a PDF re-host does not look like a rule change.
How to find it
Treat the per-room feed as presence-and-change detection, not as content proof; a 'changed' row means a human should re-read the source before relying on the table.

Q16. Can the site complete the trip cost without me entering anything?

What we have
The entries side is complete and source-linked. On the hotel side the mandatory components are now official and dated for the Venetian (resort fee $55 plus tax, $20/night registered-guest self-parking, 24-hour cancellation deadline on the published refundable rate, $150/night incidental hold) and for Wynn ($55 resort fee including parking, 48-hour offer deadline). Only the nightly room rate for these dates is missing, and no official Strip page publishes it.
What is missing
A dated quote per hotel for 6-13 December 2026 (checkout total, nightly average, mandatory fees, tax, parking, cancellation deadline, rate timestamp).
How to find it
The nightly monitor re-reads the official suite-rate page and the booking links each morning and records any published change in the dated rate log; the calculator shows the fee side and stays 'Incomplete' on the room-rate side until a dated quote exists. Nothing is asked of you.

Q17. How long will you play each event, and therefore which two events can actually be played in sequence on the same day?

What we have
Official start times and late-registration closes for every Venetian and Wynn row (structure sheets). The day schedule now tests sequencing for any playing length you enter (0.5 to 12 hours), and it shows which pairs cannot be played in sequence at that length.
What is missing
No official source publishes a finishing time or a typical playing length for any of these events, so the check depends on an estimate that only you can supply.
How to find it
Enter your own estimate in the 'Assumed playing length' box on the Day schedule page. It is never written into the data or shown as an official value. To remove the estimate, find a published finish time or a level-by-level structure sheet that implies one, and record its source.

Q18. Is an 8 a.m. start possible on these dates, and what does the day actually look like?

What we have
Answered from the published starts, not from a preference: no enterable event in any captured Strip schedule starts before 09:10, and inside 6-12 December the earliest published start is 11:00 on the first two days and 09:10 on 12 December. Restart and final-table rows are excluded because they cannot be entered. The day plan therefore starts at each day's earliest published start (see the 8 a.m. panel on the Day schedule page).
What is missing
Whether a room runs an early daily turbo that its public schedule does not list, and whether the room's own 8 a.m. cash game (which is not a tournament) belongs in the plan.
How to find it
Ask the Venetian poker room directly whether any 6:00-9:00 a.m. tournament runs on 6-12 December 2026; if none does, the plan is already the maximum: one full-field start plus an evening fallback where the registration windows allow it (F57).
