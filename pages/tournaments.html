How to read the "Single-Entry" column

Confirmed single entry = the official structure sheet states no re-entry / freezeout.

Unverified = the event is single-day ("1Day") but the official structure sheet does not state "no re-entry" or "freezeout." Re-entry may still be allowed during late registration. Confirm with the Venetian Poker Room before relying on it.

No — re-entry / multi-flight = the official structure sheet explicitly allows multiple starting flights (e.g. Day 1A/1B/1C/1D). These are listed in the separate Re-Entry / Add-On section.
