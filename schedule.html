Suggested all-day tournament schedule

Goal: enter as many live single-entry tournaments as possible, starting at 8 AM and playing all day. Times reflect typical daily tournament grids; Venetian/Wynn daily start times for multi-day series events are not yet published on official pages — treat as a template and confirm before your trip.

Important: Wynn's daily tournament schedule is suspended during Wynn Signature / Wynn Millions / Classic series; suspension during the WPT World Championship (Nov 27–Dec 21) is not stated — verify. The Venetian DeepStack daily grid is not published on the official page. Do not book travel based on this template alone; confirm with the poker rooms.

How to maximize entries

  • Stay on-site at the venue (Wynn/Encore for WPT World Championship; Venetian/Palazzo for DeepStack Extravaganza) to eliminate travel between sessions.
  • Target multi-flight events (WPT Prime Dec 5–12, WPT World Championship Dec 13–20) so you can play a Day 1 flight and, if you bust, enter a later flight — still one entry per flight.
  • Avoid Section B rebuy/add-on events if you want a pure single-entry bankroll plan.
  • Use the 10 a.m., 2 p.m., 6 p.m., 9 p.m. Caesars daily grid around the bigger multi-day events at Wynn/Venetian.
  • Venetian↔Wynn is 0.5 mi / 10–15 min walk, so you can play at both in one day.
