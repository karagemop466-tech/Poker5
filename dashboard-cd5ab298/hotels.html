Where the poker room is

Same resort doesn’t mean same elevator.

Official location details identify the poker room’s side of each property. They do not publish a timed hotel-room-to-seat route.

The Venetian / The Palazzo

Poker is at Palazzo, Level 2.

The Venetian’s official directions send guests from The Venetian lobby across the casino floor to Restaurant Row / Waterfall Atrium, then up to the Grand Canal Shoppes. From The Palazzo lobby, follow the casino floor toward the self-parking elevators and take them to Level 2. No official walking duration is published.

Official room location and directions ↗
Wynn / Encore

Poker is on the Encore casino floor.

Wynn’s official poker page places cash and tournament play in the Encore room. Wynn and Encore are one connected resort complex, but the route from any assigned hotel room depends on tower and elevator bank; no timed room-to-seat path is claimed here.

Research limit

No property-verified minute counts.

The route source is secondary. Official room-location links help orient the endpoints, but there is no verified estimate from the guest’s room door to the tournament entrance or a poker seat. A 10–15 minute casino-to-casino estimate can become longer when starting deep inside either resort.

For a poker-first stay, the clearest convenience is staying at the resort whose room is hosting the event: Venetian DSE in The Venetian’s Palazzo poker room; WPT festival at the Wynn/Encore poker room.

Optional poker-player rate

Venetian’s room offer comes with a play requirement.

The 2026 official poker-room rate guide says rates start at $119/$209 (subject to availability, blackout dates and the 150-day rate window), and directs guests to call with code EPOKR25. For each night stayed, a guest must log six hours of active live cash play or buy in to a $300+ tournament. The published direct booking quote above is a different offer and is not evidence that the poker-player rate is available for these dates.
