Screening standard

What “strict” means here.

Do not infer a freezeout from a blank field or an incomplete PDF. Confirm the event rule before spending or traveling.

To qualify as confirmed single-entry

  • The event-specific official rules say one entry per player / freezeout, or explicitly prohibit re-entry and repeat entry.
  • Multi-flight events disclose whether a player may re-enter a later flight after busting.
  • The specific date, event number and buy-in match the current calendar.

Track extra spend separately

  • A re-entry or another flight after busting breaks the one-entry cap.
  • A rebuy is more chips in the same event; it is not a second entry, but it is extra spend.
  • An add-on is not a second entry, but it is extra spend. The dashboard marks it separately rather than conflating it with re-entry.
