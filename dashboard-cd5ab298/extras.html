Entry rules · extra-chip spend

“No re-entry” is not the same as no extra spend.

This page keeps three different things distinct: buying another tournament entry, rebuying chips within an event, and taking an optional add-on. Each can change the total outlay; only re-entry or repeat flights necessarily means another entry.

Re-entry plus a $50 add-on.

Venetian multi-flight

Bagging only blocks later flights.

Event #19 and Event #26 structures say that a player who bags for Day 2 may not play a later flight. The documents do not clearly limit entries after a player busts from a flight. That is not enough to call either event single-entry.
