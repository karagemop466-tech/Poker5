- Walking times are approximate (based on Strip location) and flagged UNVERIFIED — verify with a walking-directions tool.

## How to compute the true cost (fill in after you pick dates)
1. Go to the hotel's official site and enter your exact check-in/check-out dates (within Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026).
2. Record the nightly room rate (before fees/tax).
3. Add the mandatory resort fee (confirm the exact amount on the official booking page — see source notes above).
4. Add Clark County room tax of ~13.38% (applies to rate + resort fee).
5. True cost per night = (nightly rate + resort fee) × 1.1338.
6. Total stay cost = true cost per night × number of nights (+ parking if you drive; not needed if walking).
