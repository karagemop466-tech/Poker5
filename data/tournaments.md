## Method & verification rules
- Every row below is taken from the official Venetian DSE IV schedule page and/or official Venetian structure PDFs.
- Source link is provided for every row.
- Buy-in, guarantee (GTD), date, time, event # are taken from official sources.
- "Single entry" = a (1Day) event whose official structure sheet describes ONE starting flight and contains NO re-entry / multi-flight language. These are freezeouts: one buy-in, no re-entry, no add-on. Nothing lets you buy an advantage by spending more.
- "Multi-entry / re-entry" = events with multiple Day-1 flights (1A, 1B, 1C …) plus a combined Day 2. Official structure sheets state players may play multiple flights unless they bag chips for Day 2 — i.e., spending more buy-ins can improve your chance. These are kept in a SEPARATE section (see "Tournaments where spending more can help").
- Where a value is not stated in the official source, it is left blank and flagged UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred.
