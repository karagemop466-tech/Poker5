## Entry-rule interpretation (do not overstate)

All **37** numbered PDFs referenced by the official schedule were opened and read (sheets 1–37). In the extracted rules, **no sheet explicitly says that a participant is limited to one entry in the entire event**, and no sheet explicitly confirms a re-entry, rebuy, or add-on option for the events in this series. Therefore the existence of a one-day event or silence in the PDF is **not** evidence of a freezeout. Strict single-entry remains **unverified** for every cash-entry event until the Venetian gives an explicit rule. Rebuy/add-on also remain unknown unless the event rule says otherwise.

For multi-flight events, the sheets do explicitly say a player who bags for Day 2 cannot play a subsequent flight. This constrains an additional flight after bagging but does not, by itself, establish an overall one-entry maximum or whether a player who busts may re-enter another flight. Keep those rows in the separate multi-flight category.

Where a PDF lists fees, the fee breakdown below is quoted or transcribed from the PDF. Bounty-event guarantees explicitly include bounty awards on several sheets; do not represent them as guaranteed cash-prize-pool-only amounts. Satellite guarantees are seats, not cash. A blank guarantee means the relevant PDF did not print one; consult the official schedule only where it prints one.

## Manual review points

- Schedule row links and name/price/guarantee are separate from PDF rule text; use the schedule as source for each dated flight, and link the associated PDF for structure/fees.
- A repeated schedule row can share a PDF but still differ by date, flight, late-registration time, discount eligibility, or guarantee amount; preserve event number and flight labels.
- Do not label a tournament “freezeout confirmed” based solely on a `1Day` or no-rebuy phrase. The entry cap must be explicit.
- The field “total prize pool” on bounty PDFs includes the stated bounty awards for sheets 1, 8, 11, 15, 16, 26, 27, 30, 33, and 34. Ratios use this guarantee as printed and are descriptive only; they are not forecasts or expected returns.
- Structure #19 and #24–25 are direct-seat satellites, not cash-guarantee tournaments. The seats have no official cash valuation in these sheets; leave cash guarantee blank.
