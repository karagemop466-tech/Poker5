Executive summary

Scope: single-entry poker tournaments on or after 2026-11-16 near the Las Vegas Strip, with hotels within walking distance and a daily schedule starting 8 AM. Only official sources were used for values.

Result, honestly stated: this is a partial verification. Confirmed rows come mainly from The Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV, which publishes structure sheets, plus Bellagio, Wynn, and Caesars Palace official PDFs. Several venues have no usable official schedule (Horseshoe, ARIA, MGM Grand, Planet Hollywood, Golden Nugget, Resorts World). Those gaps are left blank.
