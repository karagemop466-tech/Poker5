Questions, information & answers

Items that must be addressed before any decision can be reasonably made. Marked answered where we have information, open where data is missing, with suggestions on how to discover it.

Q1. Are Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV events truly single entry (no re-entry across flights)?
Open. The official Venetian page lists the series and guarantees but not daily start times or re-entry rules. The schedule PDF was not fetched.
How to discover: fetch https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/content/dam/vlvweb/casino/poker/deepstack/2026/dse-nov-2026-schedule.pdf and the Venetian structure sheets; call the Venetian poker room.
Q2. Does Wynn suspend its daily tournament schedule during the WPT World Championship (Nov 27–Dec 21)?
Open / partially answered. Wynn's official page states daily tournaments are suspended during Wynn Signature / Wynn Millions / Classic series. WPT World Championship is not listed — status unknown.
How to discover: call Wynn Poker Room at (702) 770-7654; check wynnpoker.com daily schedule during Nov 27–Dec 21.
Q3. What are the exact Caesars Palace daily tournament buy-ins?
Conflict. Official caesars.com says $100 buy-in for all four daily tournaments ($1,000 GTD) with a $50 add-on. Third-party reviews say $120/$160. Using official caesars.com value, flagged for review.
How to discover: call Caesars poker room; check caesars.com poker room page directly.
Q4. Are future (Nov/Dec 2026) hotel room rates bookable from official sources?
Open. Official hotel booking pages require date-specific queries and dynamic pricing; sample rates here come from third-party aggregators and are flagged unverified.
How to discover: query each hotel's official booking engine for specific dates; record all-in rates (rate + fee + tax).
Q5. MGM Grand poker data — official source unavailable (site returns HTTP 500).
Open. MGM Grand South Side Poker Showdown ($300 NLH, Nov 1–Dec 1) and daily $120/$100 NLH come via PokerAtlas (third-party). Not confirmed from an official MGM source.
How to discover: retry mgmgrandlasvegas.com; call MGM Grand poker room (702) 891-7434; check PokerAtlas as secondary only.
Q6. Are ARIA and Bellagio daily tournaments currently running, and at what times/buy-ins?
Open. Official pages list event types but say "call to confirm" (ARIA) or link to PDFs not fetched (Bellagio).
How to discover: call ARIA poker room (702) 590-7232 and Bellagio poker room (702) 693-7291; fetch linked PDF structure sheets.
Q7. Which multi-day WPT events allow multiple starting flights / re-entry?
Partially answered. WPT press release: WPT World Championship = single entry per starting flight (3 flights); WPT Prime = 4 flights; Super Gold Million = unlimited re-entry. Exact re-entry across flights needs structure sheets.
How to discover: fetch Wynn structure sheets (wynnpoker.com) for each event.
Q8. What is the total bankroll needed to play all targeted single-entry events?
Open. Cannot be computed until buy-ins, re-entry policy, and number of entries per day are confirmed.
How to discover: once Q1/Q3/Q7 answered, sum buy-ins × planned entries; add ~20% for variance.
Q9. What are the exact walking routes and times from each hotel to each venue?
Approximate. Venetian↔Wynn 0.5 mi / 10–15 min; Venetian↔Caesars ~0.7 mi / ~14 min; Venetian↔Bellagio ~0.9 mi / 18–25 min. Needs on-the-ground confirmation.
How to discover: map each route (Google Maps walking); account for Strip crossings and bridges.
Q10. Do tournament schedules change during holidays (Thanksgiving Nov 26, Christmas Dec 25, New Year)?
Open. No holiday adjustments found in sources.
How to discover: check each poker room's holiday schedule; call ahead for late-Nov/Dec dates.
