01

HOW “SINGLE-ENTRY” IS USED

One bullet, total—not one per flight.

The primary single-entry-overall list only includes an event whose current, event-specific official terms prohibit a second entry, re-entry, rebuy, later starting flight, or Day 2 entry. A schedule’s silence is not a freezeout rule. As of this research check, no upcoming event is verified to meet that standard.

Confirmed re-entry/rebuy events and events with unknown caps remain separate in the dashboard. A prior-season rule is not carried forward unless current-season materials confirm it.
