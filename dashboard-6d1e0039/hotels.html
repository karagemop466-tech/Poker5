Hotels: true cost per night

Rates are kept as a dated log rather than a single number, because the same suite on the same booking engine swings from $231.75 to $779.25 across one week. True cost per night = room rate + mandatory resort fee + Clark County lodging tax. Room rates are quoted before tax by the hotel; the resort fee is $55 per night at both the Venetian and the Wynn; the tax for a primary-gaming-corridor resort hotel is 13.38% (Clark County ordinance 4.08.130).

Baseline stay for every comparison: 2 adults, 1 room, check-in Wed 2 Dec 2026, check-out Wed 9 Dec 2026, 7 nights — the middle of the DeepStack Extravaganza IV and the first week of the WPT series, so the same dates apply to both venues.
