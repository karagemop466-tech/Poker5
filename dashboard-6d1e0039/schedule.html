All-day plan, one primary event per day

The brief asks for a schedule that fills a whole day from an early start and for each day to have a primary event plus a conditional fallback — never an impossible sequence. Start times, buy-ins and guarantees below are official; the finish times are a label-it-clearly estimate from level length (see the method note), because no room publishes end times.

Method. Estimated finish = start + (levels a one-day event typically needs × published level length) + published breaks every 3–4 levels. Levels-to-finish assumptions: 14 at 40-minute levels, 18 at 30, 20 at 25, 22 at 20. Late-registration close = start + (published late-reg level count × published level length) + breaks. A fallback is only marked feasible when its late-registration close is after the primary's estimated finish, i.e. the player must bust out early. Both figures are arithmetic on official inputs, not predictions.

The early-start problem

No official Strip page publishes an 8:00 a.m. start. Verified earliest starts:

9:10 a.m.Venetian #46, $260 turbo milestone satellite, Sat Dec 12 — official schedule
9:00 a.m.Horseshoe daily tournaments (Caesars' own article; no structure sheet retrieved) — Caesars article
10:00 a.m.Wynn Super Gold Million Mystery Bounty Day 1A/1C, Dec 10 and 11 — Wynn schedule PDF
10:10 a.m.Venetian one-day slate (Seniors, Tag Team, RaceDay) — official schedule

A realistic all-day plan therefore runs about 9:10 a.m. to midnight, or 10:10 a.m. to midnight if the player prefers the $300–$2,200 one-day events with 30–40-minute levels.
