Other Strip rooms: Bellagio, Caesars Palace

Rooms that publish recurring tournaments but do not meet the no-advantage rule. Kept separate so the strict comparison stays honest.

Why these rooms sit outside the strict list. Both publish a way to buy a bigger stack for extra money: Bellagio's $40 “voluntary staff gratuity” adds 20,000 chips, and Caesars Palace's $50 add-on adds 15,000 chips. Under the brief those are advantage purchases, so they are documented here for completeness and never ranked. Horseshoe (six daily events from 9 a.m.) and Resorts World publish schedules but no tournament structure PDF was retrievable today, so their rows are not listed instead of being guessed.
