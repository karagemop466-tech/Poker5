S01blockerNo Las Vegas Strip event is published as a true freezeout
Across all 37 Venetian structure sheets and every Wynn terms sheet that the WPT posted, not one event states “no re-entry” or “freezeout”. Two Venetian events are explicitly re-entry-priced (#18, #58: “All subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200”) and every Wynn event with a terms sheet states unlimited re-entry. The strict single-entry list is therefore empty of confirmed rows, and every candidate is shown as unverified rather than promoted.
Ask each poker room, in writing, whether the event is a freezeout. Until an official page says so, treat “unverified” rows as possibly re-entry.source 1 source 22026-11-17
S02blockerThe 8:00 a.m. start goal cannot be met on the Strip
Earliest official start found: Venetian 9:10 a.m. (event #46, Dec 12) and Venetian 10:10 a.m. for the regular slate; Wynn 10:00 a.m. (Super Gold Million, Dec 10–11); Horseshoe 9:00 a.m. (daily, per Caesars' own article). Nothing on an official page starts at 8:00 a.m.
Either accept a 9:10 a.m. first start or add an off-Strip room with a pre-9 a.m. start once verified.source 1 source 2 source 32026-11-17
S03reviewBellagio weekly event carries three different buy-ins in one PDF
The Bellagio Saturday PDF is titled “NLH $1100 BUY-IN”, is linked from the poker page as “SATURDAYS | $1,000 NLH”, and rule 07 says “Of the $600 Buy-in, $510 goes to the Prize Pool”. The same sheet has stale dates (Day 2 “9/13/26”), so the sheet appears to be an un-updated template.
Confirm the actual Saturday buy-in with the Bellagio poker room before budgeting.source 1 source 22026-11-17
S04reviewBellagio's optional $40 gratuity buys 20,000 extra chips
Rule 09: “The voluntary staff gratuity will grant players an extra 20,000 chips.” A $40 payment inside the $600 structure therefore buys a 50% larger starting stack - exactly the pay-for-advantage case the brief wants separated.
Keep Bellagio and Caesars out of the strict list; they are documented in the Advantage Events section.source 12026-11-17
S05reviewCaesars Palace advertises a paid add-on
Caesars' own article: $100 buy-in, 20,000 chips, $1,000 GTD, and “a $50 add-on at the first break gives you an extra 15,000 chips”. No official structure sheet PDF was found, so the level and late-reg details are unverified.
Request the official structure sheet; do not budget Caesars until then.source 12026-11-17
S06reviewWynn does not publish terms for events after Dec 15
The posted WPT World Championship Structures PDF ends at the $1,600 PLO Mystery Bounty ($500,000 GTD). Entry rules for the $10,400 World Championship, both $10,500 events, the $25,800 High Roller, Seniors, Ladies, Super Gold Million and the $1,000/$2,000/$3,000 NLH events are therefore blank.

S07reviewWynn schedule says World Championship registration stays open into Day 2
The schedule row for Dec 16 reads “WPT World Championship Day 2 (Registration open until the start of Day 2)”. Late registration running into Day 2 is a material difference from a normal freezeout and is recorded as an excerpt, not interpreted.
Confirm with Wynn whether a Day-2 entrant pays a fresh $10,400 and starts at a shorter stack.source 12026-11-17
S08reviewVenetian resort fee is published as “rates include” but the booking engine adds it per night
The resort-fee page says “Rates include a daily Resort Fee of $55 plus tax per night, payable upon check-in”, while the booking breakdown lists $55.00 as a separate line for each night and then applies 13.38% tax on the total.
Treat $55 + 13.38% as an unavoidable per-night charge; verify at check-in.source 1 source 2 source 32026-11-17
S09reviewLIPS events carry a flat fee that is neither prize pool nor house fee
