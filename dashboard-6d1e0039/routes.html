Walking the Strip: is the hotel really within 30 minutes?

Every hotel-to-poker-room pair used in this dashboard is listed with its door-to-door walk time. The 30-minute filter is applied and shown, and anything marginal is labelled marginal rather than rounded down to “fine”.

Source honesty. Google and Apple Maps walking directions cannot be linked with a stable, citable route, so the times below come from a published Strip walking chart that measures door-to-door routes Casino Compass walking chart. They are second-party measurements of official property layouts, not the casinos' own pages, and are labelled that way. Nothing here is presented as a Google Maps figure.

What this means for hotel choice

  • Venetian and Wynn are 10–15 minutes apart across the Sands Avenue bridge, so either property lets a player use both rooms in one trip without a taxi.
  • Bellagio is 18–25 minutes from the Venetian — inside the limit, but at the top of it when you add lobby and poker-room navigation. Treat it as marginal.
  • Resorts World is 12–18 minutes from Wynn but 25+ from the Venetian; it only works as a base if the plan centres on the Wynn.
  • Nothing south of Bellagio stays inside 30 minutes of the Venetian on foot (MGM Grand to Bellagio is 35–45 minutes), so a south-Strip hotel would break the walking rule.
